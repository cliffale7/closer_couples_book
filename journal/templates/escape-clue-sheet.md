For activity 103, The Relationship Escape Room. Two people, one agreed place, puzzles you build from personal history, knowledge of each other, and complementary skills. You solve them together. Baselines are at least 0.32 inch apart.

This sheet is a cooperative record for the room you agreed on. It does not send either person through a town or a house looking for hidden objects. Nobody is locked in. Opening the door, skipping a puzzle, or stopping for the night is part of the game. There is no rank and no forfeit.
