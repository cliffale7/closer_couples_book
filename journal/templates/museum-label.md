# Museum label

```journal
template: museum-label
activities: ["006", "102"]
titles: ["The Museum of Me", "The Museum of Unsaid Things"]
line_spacing_min_in: 0.32
min_rules_per_prompt: 8
copies: one label per exhibit
empty_case: allowed
```

One label per exhibit. Copy the sheet freely. Mark which museum. Baselines are at least 0.32 inch apart. A small card still uses that gap; if eight lines will not fit, print the label as a half or full sheet instead of compressing the type.

Activity 006, The Museum of Me: objects, people, songs, and events from a life, then a tour.

Activity 102, The Museum of Unsaid Things: a label for a thought, an appreciation, a fear, or a need left unsaid. Pair what happened with what you wish the other understood. A case may stay empty. “Not on display” is a finished label.
