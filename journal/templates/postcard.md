# Invisible postcard

```journal
template: postcard
activity: "099"
title: "The Invisible Postcards"
line_spacing_min_in: 0.32
min_rules_per_prompt: 8
copies: one sheet per moment
secret_until_exchange: true
```

For activity 099. Over several days, each of you quietly notes a moment the other helped, impressed, or delighted you. Keep each sheet to yourself until the exchange you agreed on. Then trade them. Baselines are at least 0.32 inch apart. A cut-down paper postcard still uses that gap, so it may hold only the first eight lines. Do not tighten the lines to imitate a shop postcard.

This is a note about a moment you saw. It is not a hunt for hidden objects, and it is not a tally of who noticed more.
