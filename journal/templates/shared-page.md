# Shared page

```journal
template: shared-page
line_spacing_min_in: 0.32
min_rules_per_prompt: 8
print_as: three letter sheets
on_overflow: add a sheet
fields: [what_happened, unexpected, line_worth_keeping, discovered, remembered, what_next, memento_pointer]
```

Joint page. Either person may write. Both may add. Neither rewrites the other’s sentences. Initials beside a paragraph are optional. Baselines are at least 0.32 inch apart. This sheet is three printed pages at the minimum spacing. Continuation pages repeat: Our Story, activity ID, date, attempt, and shared page.

When the companion is bound to an activity, print that ID’s shared prompt from `journal/PROMPT_BANK.md` under the header.
