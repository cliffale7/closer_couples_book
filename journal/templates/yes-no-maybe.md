# Yes, no, maybe

```journal
template: yes-no-maybe
activity: "034"
title: "The Yes, No, Maybe Game"
discretion: optional
line_spacing_min_in: 0.32
min_rows: 8
min_rules_per_narrative: 8
print_as: two letter sheets
compare_only: rows both people choose to reveal
```

For activity 034. Discreet and optional. Skip the sheet if you want. Classify romantic, affectionate, sensual, and sexual ideas as yes, no, or maybe. Write only what you are willing to reread. A category word or a private code is enough. You do not owe anyone an explicit scene.

Write before you compare. Each person fills a separate copy of this sheet. Page one holds the eight ideas. Page two holds the optional note and the compare. Bring to the compare only the rows both of you choose to reveal. A blank row is complete. A maybe is not a yes. A yes on this page is not a promise to act, and it is not consent in the moment. Either person may move a mark later; date the change and leave the old mark legible.

Baselines are at least 0.32 inch apart, including every row.
