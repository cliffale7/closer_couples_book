A letter surface for biographies, permission slips, owner’s manuals, letters from a hoped-for future, thank-yous, and promises. Any other activity may use it when prose wants a page of its own. Baselines are at least 0.32 inch apart. 16 lines fit one letter sheet with the header below. Continue on another sheet of 16 rather than narrowing the gap.

The letter can stay private, be read aloud, or be shared later. Say which. Do not write down what you would rather leave spoken.
