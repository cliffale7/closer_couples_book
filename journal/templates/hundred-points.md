For activity 106. Each person spends 100 imaginary points across forms of closeness they name themselves. Then you design an evening both of you find appealing. Points are a toy for seeing appetite. They are not a bill, a debt, or consent. Any piece of the evening can be declined in the moment. A large number next to a form does not obligate the other person.

Name your own forms. Words you may ignore: a long talk, a walk, cooking, sitting quietly, playful nonsense, affectionate touch. Leave a row blank. Put a form on a private copy of this sheet if you do not want it on the shared page.

Both columns have eight rows and the same spacing. Baselines are at least 0.32 inch apart.
