# Owner-facing art, typography and physical-book review

Keep the current **Night Library** midnight/parchment/copper style until the owner asks for a different aesthetic. Art agents should *curate*, not use a complete restyle as an excuse to redo everything or reduce the page count.

## Required visual proof gallery

Provide labeled contact sheets and high-resolution individual page images for: both covers, 13 part opener spreads, at least 24 chapter openings representing all 13 parts and unique mechanics, six signatures, two long introspective chapters, at least three game/puzzle sheets, one intimate/sensual exercise, independent A/B/shared journal pages, revisit/memento sheets, all index styles, a bibliography and colophon, editable DOCX image treatment and reflowing EPUB reading samples.

Also create a complete `ART_AUDIT.md` inventory for all 108 motifs, 13 part openers, six signature heroes and covers, with `KEEP / TECHNICAL_FIX / AESTHETIC_RECOMMENDATION`, and why. Samples must be legible without zooming so far that users can't judge composition. Never portray line-art path uniqueness alone as artistic originality or quality.

## Decisions the owner can make (not agent-assumed approvals)

- Does the restrained line-art style feel warm and distinctive enough, or does it need richer spot illustrations/textures in specific sections?
- Is the full page format comfortable and gorgeous enough to live with for years, not merely readable once?
- Does the book feel mature, funny and serious at the right moments, or too clinically uniform?
- Are the page backgrounds, writing lines, font size and visual breaks comfortable for actual pen-on-paper use?
- Can a puzzle/letter/eye-gazing chapter be followed without searching other volumes/files?
- Are all covers and signature artworks appropriate for a very large keepsake volume?

## Physical proof check if printing available

Print a chapter page, one part opener, one partner journal page, a shared continuation, two cards and the longer 103 grid/key separation at actual 100% size. Check line spacing with a ruler (≥0.32 in per baseline), legible body text, trim/margins/inner gutter, grayscale rules/contrast, cards/cutting, solution-page privacy, page order. Record measured values and photographs only with owner permission. If physical printing is unavailable, state 'pending physical proof' rather than inventing measurements.

**No book-shrinking decisions belong in this phase.**
