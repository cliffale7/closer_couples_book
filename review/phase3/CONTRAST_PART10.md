## Why a swap fails

**073 / 080.** 073 seals two full drafts, then marks same memory, news, and remembered differently. The finished uncertainty is allowed to stay in the origin story. 080 hides only the date. The day is built one band at a time where both people can see it, and the extra object is the refusal to restage a part. Swapping them would either unveil a finished essay, which 080 no longer does, or turn the beginning into a four-band afternoon, which is not 073.

**074 / 078.** 074 trades underestimated impact one item at a time and stores agreed pairs in two columns. 078 delivers one thanks, invites a correction of the cost, and then asks whether any sentence may be kept. A list of weights the doer missed is not a belated thank-you with a storage decision.

**075 / 079.** 075 sorts stories into four columns and marks keepers before anyone mentions a song as the point. 079 names the piece before playback, allows silence, and is unfinished until one story survives with the sound off. A set list with the player closed is 075. A soundtrack that can lose its file is 079.

**076 / 073.** Both use a private sketch, then one person speaks. 076's listener waits a minute and reflects a change of picture, and does not have to agree or perform it. 073's listener marks agreement, news, and mismatch during the reading, then compares the two beginnings. A revised portrait is not an origin draft.

**077 / 080.** 077 is many years on one shared line, including a challenge and a disputed date kept as two notes. 080 is one date, chosen in hiding, then four bands and a refusal. A life map is not a day clock.

**075 / 080.** 075 finishes a repertoire you might tell again. 080 finishes one day's clock and a part you are not asking life to repeat. A favorite story in the meaningful column is not a day rebuilt in order.
