## Why a swap fails

**001 / 005 / 081.** 001 is a one-sided interview: one question, two follow-ups, the interviewer does not answer, equal timed turns, then a dulled fact. Materials are two seats, a visible timer, and paper. 005 deals a shuffled six-card deck and will not draw the next card until the asker reflects one concrete detail. Materials are blank cards, a pen, and a timer. 081 never lets the asker choose in the moment: a coin opens, the bowl decides, and the drawn person may answer, pass, or offer the slip back. Materials are the printed bank or blank paper, two pens, a bowl, and a coin. Playing one procedure under another title would drop the stranger turn, the per-card echo, or the chance-and-destroy cap.

**041 / 107.** 041 asks for one guess about which of three whole stories is invented, then restates the two true scenes. Materials are three matched cards, a pen, a timer, and a Keep slip. 107 hides one load-bearing detail, allows three yes-or-no guesses, and ends on the kind of symbol. Materials are optional: one slip for that detail. Guessing which story is fake is not the same as hunting a seam and naming a wish, a fear, or a possibility.

**039 / 046.** 039 writes a meeting of the real people and forbids a plot. The listener has two sentences and no critique. There is no extended version, because a second era belongs to 046. Materials are paper or a notes app. 046 starts from a world menu and job titles, trades sentences through a mishap, and requires a repair inside the fiction before anyone names a trait. Materials are a timer and optional paper. A costume meeting with no disaster is 039. A caper that brings home a trait is 046.

**006 / 102.** 006's visitor asks one question per gallery so the piece can be seen more clearly, and both people give a tour. A real object is optional; a timer is part of the setup. 102's curator, after one question, says whether the label is to be carried, was only for the saying, or is not on display. Symmetry is optional. Materials are cards and a pen, with no timer and no object-person-song-event set. A life tour cannot be played as a pocket of unsaid things, and uncarried cards in 102 do not stay on the wall.

## Overlap that remains, and why it did not force a rewrite

The pairs share a genre: questions, spotting an invention, another life, a museum. They do not share a first move, a material set, or a final artifact. Hooks already point at the neighbor. Rewriting The experience for tone would not make the procedures more distinct.
