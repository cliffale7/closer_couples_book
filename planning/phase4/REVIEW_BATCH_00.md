# Independent review — sample batch

Reviewer role: visual review only. The same session that requested the pictures also opened the files and judged them. A later batch should be read again the same way, from the image, before a path in the book changes.

## Cover, first attempt — reject

File: `assets/illustrations/phase4/review/cover-victorian-rejected.jpg`.

Two adults at a long table, copper lamp, night window, parchment wall. Faces are distinct. Hands meet in a clasp that reads as hands. The room and the light match the palette. The clothing is Victorian: a high-collared dress, a cravat, a period chair. The book is a contemporary couples' workbook. This plate would make the edition look like a historical novel. It is not used.

## Cover, second attempt — accept

File: `assets/covers/cover-front.jpg` (864×1152).

A man in a dark coat and a woman in a knit sweater and burgundy coat sit at a small present-day table. A copper dome lamp sits between them. The window shows a night city. Faces are specific, eyes meet, and the visible hands rest in plausible places: one on the table edge, one near a lap. No text, no watermark. Adult, clothed, quiet. Print note: about 196 ppi at the 4.4-inch cover width used in the workbook. Good enough to review on the page. Not a 300 ppi full-bleed wrap.

## Chapter 001 — accept

File: `assets/chapters/001/illustration.jpg` (1280×720).

Chapter: The Stranger Across From Me. Two adults across a small café table at night, two cups, a lamp, city lights. The pose is an interview, which is the chapter. The hand at the chin and the hands on the table read as hands. Clothing is contemporary. No text. At 5.4 inches wide this is about 237 ppi.
