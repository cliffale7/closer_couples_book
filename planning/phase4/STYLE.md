# Style lock — anime campfire

Status: locked 17 October 2026. Replaces the lamp-lit painted edition. The owner accepted the campfire picture as the character reference.

Reference file: `assets/illustrations/phase4/character-reference.jpg`.

Clean modern anime. Playful, adult, fully clothed. Not a photograph, not oil-paint realism, not Victorian costume, and not the rejected line drawings.

Erica has long blond hair with frosted tips and green eyes. Clifford has brown hair with gray in it, tied back, bright blue eyes, and a goatee only: mustache and chin patch, cheeks and jaw clean-shaven. The husky wears a padded pink vest harness. The small white dog wears a thin pink strap harness and has a brown patch over its right eye only. The fur around its left eye stays white.

Book plates use these people when a scene has people. Dogs appear when the scene has room for them. The small dog is female. A brown patch covers only her right eye. The fur around her left eye stays white. Erica's cup always shows a unicorn and no words. Clifford's cup always shows the words NAMASTE, BITCHES and nothing else. No campground sign, no watermark, and no other lettering.

Chapter and part plates are 16:9. Covers and the title image are 3:4. A plate is placed only after a look at the file for likeness, narrative fit, anatomy, and a clean goatee.
