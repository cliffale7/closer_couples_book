# Style lock — lamp-lit table

Status: locked for Phase 4 from the accepted samples, 15 October 2026.

Painted editorial illustration. Warm parchment walls, midnight-blue night, copper light, burgundy cloth, a little teal in the shadow. Visible paint, not a photograph, not a cartoon, and not glossy stock romance.

Adults are contemporary people in their forties, fully clothed, with believable faces and hands. Intimacy is attention, proximity, and ordinary rooms. No explicit sex. No Victorian or costume-drama dress. No stick figures, icons, geometric line art, letters, words, watermarks, or signatures. The book sets the title in type.

Chapter and part plates use a 16:9 frame and print at 5.4 inches wide. The cover uses a 3:4 frame. When a chapter is an object lesson, paint the objects in a real room. When a chapter is two people, paint two people doing that hour's action.

Prompt stem for every later plate:

> Painted editorial book illustration, fully rendered, recognizable scene. Contemporary adults in their forties when people appear, fully clothed, believable faces and hands. Warm parchment light, midnight-blue shadows, copper lamp light, burgundy and a little teal. Intimate and adult, not explicit. Not a photograph, not a cartoon, not glossy stock. No stick figures, no icons, no geometric line art, no letters, no words, no watermark, no signature, no Victorian costume.

A plate is approved only after a separate look at the file for visual quality, narrative fit, adult sophistication, anatomy, composition, and the print-size limit above.
