# House style

The voice is a sharp, warm friend who has read the research and still likes a joke. Speak to two capable adults who already like each other.

- Use contractions. Prefer concrete nouns and verbs.
- Do not open chapters with “In today’s busy world” or “Communication is the key.”
- Do not promise that an exercise will strengthen, heal, or save the relationship.
- Name the mechanism: familiarity hides questions; being accurately imagined feels different from being praised; novelty gives you a new view of someone you know.
- Humor is welcome. Meanness, humiliation, and coerced disclosure are not.
- Sensual chapters are candid. Name desire, bodies, and boundaries in plain language. Every physical step can be declined in the moment.
- “Confession Without Consequence” means a receptive style of listening. It is not immunity for harm.
- Prefer “you two” or “one of you / the other.” Do not assign roles by gender.
- Oxford comma. One space after periods. Em dashes sparingly.
- Numbers: spell one through nine in prose; use numerals for minutes, scores, and IDs.
- Chapter titles stay exactly as cataloged, including curly apostrophes when the catalog uses them.
- Examples live in a clearly labeled fictional pair, “Rowan and Vale,” used only as illustration. They are not the readers.
