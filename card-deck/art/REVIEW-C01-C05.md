# Art review, first five

Reviewer looked at the image files, not only the prompts. Status below is an editorial art check before the independent auditor sees the printed cards. It is not owner approval.

Native size of each file: 864 by 1152 pixels. Placed at 2.88 by 3.84 inches, that is 300 ppi of actual source pixels. Nothing was upscaled.

| Card | Scene | Decision | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C01 | Two adults, blank card, round table | Accept | Hands readable. No text in the picture. Faces are consistent and adult. |
| C02 | Night kitchen, one speaking, one listening, closed book | Accept | Distinct from C01. Fingers on the speaking hand read as a plausible open gesture. |
| C03 | Sofa, two blank slips about to be traded | Accept | Period dress is heavier than C01. Still the same ink-and-wash language. Blank paper stays blank. |
| C04 | Mug, record, coat, blank ticket, two people looking down | Accept | The still life matches the museum card. The pointing hand is readable. |
| C05 | Window at night, one explaining, one offering an open hand | Accept | Not an argument. Moon and city stay free of lettering. |

These five are not the smoke-test image. Each file is a separate generation.

Known limit: the generator has no pixel-size control, so a full-bleed 3.5-inch picture would be about 247 ppi. The layout keeps the picture at 2.88 inches wide so the print uses the real 300 ppi instead of pretending an enlargement added detail.
