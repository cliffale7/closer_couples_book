Breathe With Me

Sit together and let breathing do what it does. Your own rate is the whole technique.

  1. Seats first. Say: we are not trying to breathe alike. We are sitting here until the breath does whatever it does. The word here is optional.
  2. Start six minutes. Quit performing a slow breath. Do not correct the other person.
  3. Sighs and shifts are allowed. Move if you are in pain. Eyes may stay open.
  4. Wait a moment when it ends. Two sentences each: what you noticed in yourself, then one thing you could point to.

The standard sit is six minutes, inside about fifteen. Claim no sync.

Never restrict the breath, and never hold it. Stop for pain, dizziness, or distress. Agree any hand before the timer. This is not a prelude to sex.

Speak or stop at any time. Stopping is success. A recline is a full version.
