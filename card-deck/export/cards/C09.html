Vulnerability and the Courage to Be Seen

When I Feel Small

Name when you feel inadequate or overlooked, and the response that has actually helped.

  1. About 8 private minutes. Two recognizable situations. For each, one helping behavior and one that bounces. "Love me more" is too vague. Unsure is allowed. A guess is labeled a guess.
  2. Share one situation and both responses, as written. Your partner repeats the help in their words, then says can, sometimes, or not really. No demonstration unless you ask.
  3. Correct them if they polished your sentence into a nicer one. Then swap. Stop after one situation each.

Done when the help has been repeated and answered with a real limit.

If the situation is a harmful pattern, they restate and do not cross-examine. Stopping it is a separate conversation.

You may pass, shrink a situation, or leave what bounces unspoken. A pass finishes the turn.

about 30 minutes No touch required. Book 015
