Vulnerability and the Courage to Be Seen

Confession Without Consequence

Offer one harmless embarrassment, regret, insecurity, or private oddity. Curious questions only.

  1. Name the four kinds. Agree a pause word. "Different item" is enough.
  2. Before the story, say, "This is mine to tell, and it is harmless," and name the kind. If you cannot, it leaves the game. Tell it in 3 to 5 minutes.
  3. Two curious questions, not a case and not advice. A declined question is replaced, or you stop at one.
  4. Close the turn. Swap with equal time. The second person may pass, and does not owe a more dramatic kind.

Done when each allowed offer, or a pass, has been heard inside the rule.

If harm shows up, say, "This leaves the game." Abuse, a betrayal they have a right to know, an undisclosed health or legal risk, harm to someone else, infidelity, and illegal acts stay outside.

Either of you may pass or decline a question. The pause word changes the item.
