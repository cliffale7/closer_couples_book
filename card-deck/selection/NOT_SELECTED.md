# Not selected for the 65

Forty-three chapters stay in the book. They were not weak. They lost a seat because another exercise in the same part made a clearer standalone card, or because the full version needs props, a long printed bank, or a second session the card cannot honestly hold.

The archived 67-card trial remains in `card-deck/archive/trial-67/`. It is not this inventory. This list drops two workshop cards from that trial and changes several seats so repair, disagreement, and a wish behind a complaint are in the deck.

## Part 1

- 003 The Map of My Day. A daily map is useful and less of an event than a stranger's questions or an untold episode.
- 004 The Room You Grew Up In. Rich, and slower to enter than the museum of objects.
- 008 The Compliment I Don't Believe. Close to 030, which is the cleaner receiving practice.

## Part 2

- 010 The Fear I Don't Talk About. 009 already holds one difficult admission. Two fear cards would crowd the section.
- 011 The Mask I Wear. Overlaps the armor card and the permission slip.
- 014 The Part of Me That Still Hurts. Real, and better kept as a chapter than a first-deck card beside 009.

## Part 3

- 018 The Story I Tell Myself About Us. Reflective, and less embodied than the look, the breath, and the listening clock.
- 021 The Question I Have Been Saving. A saved question is good. 005 and 081 already carry question games.
- 024 The Silence We Can Share. Quiet closeness is already represented by 017 and 019.

## Part 4

- 029 The Way You Say My Name. Tender and narrow. The instructions and the avalanche cover more of being chosen.
- 031 The Love Letter You Can Read Aloud. A letter wants more paper than a card back.
- 032 The Small Devotion. Close to 074's unnoticed moments, which sit in the story section.

## Part 5

- 035 The Fantasy You Can Tell. 037 already carries one spoken wish, with a restatement.
- 039 The Kiss, Considered. A single technique card. The sensory date and the slow dance travel farther.
- 040 The Morning After the Conversation. A follow-up, not a first draw.

## Part 6

- 043 The Newlywed Game Without the Wedding. Fun, and it needs a longer question bank than the debate and the truths.
- 046 The Alternate-Universe Couple. Imaginative, and less instantly playable than the role reversal.
- 048 The Secret Mission Exchange. A between-meeting mission. The deck favors one-sitting cards.

## Part 7

- 050 The Belief I Inherited. Strong. 007 already handles a changed mind, and 089 handles imported assumptions.
- 052 The Version of Home I Want. Overlaps the future section's agreements.
- 054 The Thing I Protect. Close to 086's kept-small opinion and 108's door.

## Part 8

- 061 The Repair I Still Owe Myself. Self-repair matters. 060 is the card two people can play tonight.
- 062 The Boundary, Said Once. A boundary chapter. 063 already turns irritation into a declinable request.
- 064 The Trust Bank. A ledger. The heard-versus-meant card is the sharper trust exercise.

## Part 9

- 069 The Place We Have Not Been. A destination card. The twenty-dollar plan is more usable this week.
- 070 The Skill I Want to Learn Beside You. Good, and the first-time challenge already covers beginner novelty.
- 071 The Day With No Errand. Gentle. The random-decision night is the more distinctive adventure.

## Part 10

- 077 The Timeline We Don't Share. Useful, and heavier than a first story card. The first chapter already allows two beginnings.
- 079 The Photograph I Keep Explaining. Needs a picture in the room.
- 080 The Story We Tell Other People. Social performance. The greatest-hits card is the one you play together.

## Part 11

- 083 The Secret Nobody Would Guess. Near 013's harmless confession.
- 084 The Ordinary Mystery. Near 087, which has the clearer staircase of questions.
- 085 What Would You Do. Scenario play. 082 already holds the moral collision.

## Part 12

- 092 The Season We Are In. A check-in. The weather report in the workshop is the sharper forecast.
- 094 The Letter to the Couple We Are Becoming. A letter. 090 does the future in a form that fits a card.
- 096 The Ritual We Have Not Named. Close to 093's ordinary repeatable goals.

## Part 13

- 097 The Deck of Ordinary Oracles. Would blur the deck's rule that these cards are activities, not divination.
- 098 The Conversation Menu. A menu of other chapters, not a standalone experience.
- 099 The Two-Minute Origin. A tiny origin. 073 is the fuller first chapter.
- 102 The Rules of This House. Overlaps 089.
- 103 The Escape Room for Two. Needs sealed packets and a key kept from the players. It cannot be one honest card.
- 104 The Two-Person Civilization. A long build. 106 negotiates closeness more directly.
- 107 The Annotated Evening. A review of a night. 105's decoder is the more surprising workshop game.
