03 · Closeness

Breathe With Me

Settle the seats before you start.

  1. Say “here” if you want one word. Start a timer for six minutes.
  2. Breathe at your own rate. Matching is optional. Do not correct the other person’s breathing.
  3. Sighs and small shifts are allowed. Stop if you are in pain.
  4. When the timer ends, or either of you stops, wait a moment. Then one sentence each about what you noticed in yourself.

Remember: the length, and one notice. Skip any claim that you synced.

Either of you may end the sit. Ending is a finished turn.

05 · Desire

Yes, No, Maybe

Two private sheets. No shared screen until the stars are chosen. You are sorting ideas, not doing them tonight.

  1. Apart, head four lanes: romantic, affectionate, sensual, sexual. A blank lane is allowed. Add or cross out any idea.
  2. Still apart, star up to five yeses you will discuss, and up to three nos you want known. Unstarred nos stay unseen.
  3. Together, read one starred item at a time. The listener says “heard,” or asks one question, then stops.
  4. Name at most one easy shared yes, and say aloud that you still ask and nothing else is scheduled. Or name none.

A star is not consent. A maybe is not a promise. Skip any lane. If either of you has been drinking, play another night.

08 · Trust

The Gentle Disagreement

Agree that “pause” stops the topic. Breakups, money crises, jealousy, and in-law emergencies are off the list tonight.

  1. Name one current preference, chore, or plan in a single sentence. If it needs a history, choose a smaller difference.
  2. Each of you: your preference, then the care under it. Two or three sentences. Nobody has to concede.
  3. Each of you asks one curious question. The other answers. A question that builds a case is retired.
  4. If a one-week experiment appears, write it, including what pauses it. Willing, willing with an edit, or unwilling. Unwilling finishes the card.

Remember one line of the other person’s care, if you want a note.

Say “pause” and you both stop. A stopped topic has no penalty and no make-up debate tonight.
