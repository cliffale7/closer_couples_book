# Trial deck — scan this first

Status: proposed, 26 October 2026. No pictures yet. Sixty-seven cards for approval before a night with Erica.

Each part is its own color. Pick up a stack. Do not shuffle the colors together.

The sentence under each title is the invitation already in that chapter. It is the book's own summary, not a rewrite.

Passing is a finished turn. A card is not consent to touch or sex.

## 01  Knowing · Copper

The Art of Really Knowing Someone

- **001** The Stranger Across From Me · 20–45 minutes
  Interview the person you already know as if you had just met, and let a courteous question reach a fact familiarity had filed away.

- **002** The Life You’ve Never Told Me · 20–45 minutes
  Tell one episode they have never heard in this detail, and keep the turn and the person who shaped you inside the scene.

- **005** The Things Nobody Thinks to Ask · 20–45 minutes
  Write unusual, specific questions you have never asked each other, then reflect one true detail before the next card.

- **006** The Museum of Me · 20–45 minutes
  Curate four exhibits from a life, an object, a person, a song, and an event, and give your partner the tour.

- **007** The Opinion I Changed · 20–45 minutes
  Tell a conviction you once held strongly, how it changed, and what that change shows about the way you grow.

## 02  Courage · Burgundy

Vulnerability and the Courage to Be Seen

- **009** The Uncomfortable Truth · 20–45 minutes
  Say something that is actually hard to admit, then let it sit for 3 minutes without a repair.

- **012** The Permission Slip · 20–45 minutes
  Sign your own permission to be unfinished in one specific way, and let your partner hear it without granting it.

- **013** Confession Without Consequence · 20–45 minutes
  Offer one harmless embarrassment, regret, insecurity, or private oddity for curious listening, and keep harm outside the game.

- **015** When I Feel Small · 20–45 minutes
  Name the moment you feel inadequate or overlooked, and the response that actually helps.

- **016** No Armor Tonight · 10–20 minutes
  For a few minutes, set down the joke-as-cover, the defense, and the impressive answer.

## 03  Closeness · Teal

The Science and Experience of Closeness

- **017** Four Minutes of Forever · 10–20 minutes
  Four quiet minutes of mutual looking, with the love myth left at the door.

- **019** Breathe With Me · 10–20 minutes
  Sit together and let breathing settle by itself, with nothing to match and nothing to fix.

- **020** The Listening Experiment · 20–45 minutes
  One person finishes a meaningful turn. The other gives it back in words that can be corrected.

- **022** The Language of Touch · 20–45 minutes · touch
  A private vote on welcome, nonsexual touch, and nothing outside the overlap.

- **023** The Sixty-Second Embrace · 10–20 minutes · touch
  One deliberate embrace, timed because this book said so, kept only while it stays comfortable.

## 04  Chosen · Rose

Love, Affection, and Feeling Chosen

- **025** What Loving Me Actually Looks Like · 20–45 minutes
  Two lists of visible acts, read side by side, with no type and no score.

- **026** The Appreciation Avalanche · 20–45 minutes
  Three rounds of thanks, each one harder to aim at a stranger.

- **027** The Moment I Knew · 20–45 minutes
  The story of when they started to matter more, thunderbolt optional.

- **028** Instructions for Loving Me · 45–90 minutes
  A short, playful manual for how you run, written by you and due to expire.

- **030** The Compliment You Can’t Dodge · 20–45 minutes
  One precise compliment, 10 quiet seconds, and a thank-you that stays put.

## 05  Desire · Plum

Desire, Attraction, and Sensual Intimacy

- **033** The Attraction Interview · 20–45 minutes
  Ask what caught you first, what catches you on a Tuesday, and which old theory of chemistry you have retired.

- **034** The Yes, No, Maybe Game · 45–90 minutes
  Sort romantic, affectionate, sensual, and sexual ideas alone, then compare only what you star.

- **036** The Sensory Date · 45–90 minutes · touch
  Sequence sound, scent, texture, taste, and light, and add only the touch you have both marked welcome.

- **037** The Desire Nobody Asked About · 20–45 minutes
  Name a wish you have never asked for, and let it stay a wish.

- **038** The Slow Dance With No Music · 20–45 minutes · touch
  Sway together in silence, with no steps to get right.

## 06  Play · Amber

Playfulness, Laughter, and a Little Mischief

- **041** Two Truths and a Beautiful Lie · 20–45 minutes
  Three short stories, one of them a generous fiction, and a second look at the two that really happened.

- **042** Would You Rather: Unhinged Edition · 20–45 minutes
  A forced choice between two invented options, a one-sentence reason, and a third door if the question is a cage.

- **044** The Ridiculous Role Reversal · 20–45 minutes
  Play each other, lightly and too much, then let the person who was played sort the joke.

- **045** The Great Debate of Absolutely Nothing · 20–45 minutes
  Defend an empty proposition with a straight face, then set the opinion down in an ordinary voice.

- **047** Truth, Dare, or Discover · 20–45 minutes
  On your turn, choose a truth, a funny dare, or a tiny discovery, and count a pass as a finished move.

## 07  Inner · Indigo

The Inner World: Dreams, Meaning, and Identity

- **049** The Life Not Taken · 20–45 minutes
  One real fork, an ordinary day on the other side, and the desire that never quite left.

- **052** The Belief Beneath the Belief · 20–45 minutes
  One conviction you actually hold, told in three layers: where it came from, why it matters, and what might revise it.

- **053** If Nobody Were Watching · 20–45 minutes
  Name the audiences, send them outside, and then say what you would pursue, express, or abandon.

- **055** What I Want to Experience Before I Die · 45–90 minutes
  A personal list with Tuesday on it as well as the far-off adventure, witnessed, and not co-owned.

- **056** The Questions Without Answers · 20–45 minutes
  Six open questions, a blank if you need one, and a closing move that refuses the last word.

## 08  Trust · Slate

Trust, Communication, and Emotional Safety

- **057** The Instruction Manual for My Bad Days · 20–45 minutes
  Write the way a hard day looks on you, and the kinds of help that actually help.

- **058** What You Heard vs. What I Meant · 20–45 minutes
  Set the sentence you meant beside the sentence they heard, story and all.

- **060** The Repair Shop · 20–45 minutes
  Take one cooled, minor misstep through acknowledgment, your own part, and a repair that fits.

- **061** The Invisible Need · 20–45 minutes
  Name the need that was riding inside your own joke, silence, or short answer.

- **062** The Safety Map · 20–45 minutes
  Map the actions, situations, and words that help you open, and the ones that bring your guard up.

## 09  Adventure · Forest

Shared Adventures and New Experiences

- **065** The First-Time Challenge · 45–90 minutes
  Do one mild thing neither of you has done, then see what the hour was like and what faded.

- **066** The Twenty-Dollar Adventure · 45–90 minutes
  Build one small outing under a twenty-dollar ceiling, or under none, and see what you invent.

- **067** The Comfort Zone Exchange · 45–90 minutes
  Host a small piece of something you know, then switch and be the beginner.

- **068** The Night of Random Decisions · 90 minutes–3 hours
  Let a die, a coin, or a card choose among options you both already accepted, and keep a veto in your pocket.

- **070** The World Through Your Eyes · 45–90 minutes
  Each of you hosts a small, real piece of something that matters, and says why it matters.

## 10  Story · Gold

The Past, the Present, and the Story of Us

- **073** The First Chapter · 20–45 minutes
  Write the beginning alone, then lay the two drafts side by side and keep what does not match.

- **074** The Moments You Didn’t Know Mattered · 20–45 minutes
  Name a small gesture whose impact outran anything the giver thought they had done.

- **075** Our Greatest Hits · 45–90 minutes
  Four kinds of shared story, compiled as a set list you might actually play again.

- **076** The Moment I Saw You Differently · 20–45 minutes
  One scene that broke a smaller picture, and the wider picture you hold now.

- **078** The Unspoken Thank-You · 20–45 minutes
  One thanks that never got finished: the act, the cost as you understand it, and the difference it made.

## 11  Curiosity · Ember

Radical Curiosity and the Surprising Stuff

- **081** The Forbidden Question Box · 20–45 minutes
  Rare questions go in privately, chance draws them, and a pass finishes the turn.

- **083** The Secret Nobody Would Guess · 20–45 minutes
  One true fact that complicates an assumption, volunteered, with nobody guessing first.

- **084** The Dark Side of Me · 20–45 minutes
  One unflattering tendency, a recent small example, owned by the person who has it.

- **087** The Question Behind the Question · 20–45 minutes
  Start with an ordinary answer, then follow it down a few honest steps.

- **088** The Thing I’m Still Figuring Out · 20–45 minutes
  Name a question you cannot honestly answer tonight, and keep each other company beside it.

## 12  Future · Sage

Building a Closer Future Together

- **089** The Relationship We Would Invent · 45–90 minutes
  Name the scripts you inherited, then draft a few agreements you can picture this month.

- **090** The Future Memory · 20–45 minutes
  Tell one happy scene as if it has already happened, then look at the bridge back to this month.

- **091** What We Want More Of · 20–45 minutes
  Name the presences you want to grow, then try one shared and one that is yours alone.

- **093** The Relationship Bucket List · 45–90 minutes
  A short shared menu of adventures, traditions, achievements, and goals that are ridiculous on purpose.

- **095** A Promise I Actually Want to Keep · 20–45 minutes
  One to three promises you would still want if nobody applauded, each with a follow-through you control.

## 13  Workshop · Ink

The Inventor's Workshop: Original Proposals

- **100** The Emotional Weather Report · 5–10 minutes
  A short inner forecast, plus an optional line about tomorrow that the weatherperson keeps.

- **101** The Borrowed Brain Experiment · 20–45 minutes
  Guess the reason your partner would move, and let a lucky yes stay worthless if the reason is wrong.

- **104** The Two-Person Civilization · 45–90 minutes
  Found ten laws for a country of two, then let a shuffled event force you to revise or defend them.

- **105** The Compliment Decoder · 10–20 minutes
  Specific praise, a guess at the scene behind it, and a reveal that may name a different day.

- **106** The Intimacy Negotiators · 20–45 minutes
  Spend exactly 100 imaginary points so an evening can be built from overlap, limits included.

- **107** The Memory Smuggler · 20–45 minutes
  A true story with one invented detail, and that detail has to stand for a wish, a fear, or a chance you did not take.

- **108** The Secret Door · 45–90 minutes
  A door the other person may not have known was there, and a key they cannot demand.
