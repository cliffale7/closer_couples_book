# 108  The Secret Door

**Workshop** · Ink · 45–90 minutes · low energy · deep

## Invitation

A door the other person may not have known was there, and a key they cannot demand.

## First move

Each of you names a door the other may not know is there. You may describe it. You do not have to hand over the key.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
