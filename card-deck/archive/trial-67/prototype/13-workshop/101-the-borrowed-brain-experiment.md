# 101  The Borrowed Brain Experiment

**Workshop** · Ink · 20–45 minutes · medium energy · personal

## Invitation

Guess the reason your partner would move, and let a lucky yes stay worthless if the reason is wrong.

## First move

Guess the reason your partner would make one ordinary choice. They say whether the reason is right. A lucky yes is worthless if the reason is wrong.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
