# 104  The Two-Person Civilization

**Workshop** · Ink · 45–90 minutes · medium energy · personal

## Invitation

Found ten laws for a country of two, then let a shuffled event force you to revise or defend them.

## First move

Together, write five laws for a country of two. Then draw one small event and decide whether a law bends.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
