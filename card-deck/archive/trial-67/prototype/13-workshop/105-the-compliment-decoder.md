# 105  The Compliment Decoder

**Workshop** · Ink · 10–20 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Specific praise, a guess at the scene behind it, and a reveal that may name a different day.

## First move

Give one specific compliment. Your partner guesses the scene behind it. Then you reveal the day you actually meant, which may be a different day.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
