# 020  The Listening Experiment

**Closeness** · Teal · 20–45 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

One person finishes a meaningful turn. The other gives it back in words that can be corrected.

## First move

One of you talks for a few minutes about something that matters. The other gives it back in words that can be corrected. Then switch.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
