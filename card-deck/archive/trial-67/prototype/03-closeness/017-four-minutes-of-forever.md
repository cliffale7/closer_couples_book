# 017  Four Minutes of Forever

**Closeness** · Teal · 10–20 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Four quiet minutes of mutual looking, with the love myth left at the door.

## First move

Sit where you can see each other. Look, quietly, for four minutes. You may blink and you may smile. You do not have to perform love.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
