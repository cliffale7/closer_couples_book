# 068  The Night of Random Decisions

**Adventure** · Forest · 90 minutes–3 hours · medium energy · light

## Invitation

Let a die, a coin, or a card choose among options you both already accepted, and keep a veto in your pocket.

## First move

Agree on a few acceptable options. Let a coin, a die, or a card choose among them. Either of you may veto.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
