# 070  The World Through Your Eyes

**Adventure** · Forest · 45–90 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Each of you hosts a small, real piece of something that matters, and says why it matters.

## First move

Each of you shows one real thing that matters to you, in the world or in the room, and says why. The other does not rate it.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
