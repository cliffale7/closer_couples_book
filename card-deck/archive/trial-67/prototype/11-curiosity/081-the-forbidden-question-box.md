# 081  The Forbidden Question Box

**Curiosity** · Ember · 20–45 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Rare questions go in privately, chance draws them, and a pass finishes the turn.

## First move

Each of you puts two rare questions in a box, privately. Draw by chance. A pass finishes the turn.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
