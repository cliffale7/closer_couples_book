# 012  The Permission Slip

**Courage** · Burgundy · 20–45 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Sign your own permission to be unfinished in one specific way, and let your partner hear it without granting it.

## First move

Write one sentence that gives you permission to be unfinished in a specific way. Read it aloud. Your partner hears it. They do not grant it.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
