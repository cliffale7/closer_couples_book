# 013  Confession Without Consequence

**Courage** · Burgundy · 20–45 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Offer one harmless embarrassment, regret, insecurity, or private oddity for curious listening, and keep harm outside the game.

## First move

Offer one harmless embarrassment, odd habit, or small regret. The listener stays curious. Leave harm, and anyone outside the two of you, out of the game.
