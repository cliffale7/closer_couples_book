# 005  The Things Nobody Thinks to Ask

**Knowing** · Copper · 20–45 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Write unusual, specific questions you have never asked each other, then reflect one true detail before the next card.

## First move

Each of you writes three questions you have never asked. Trade one. Answer it. Reflect one true detail before the next card.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
