# 006  The Museum of Me

**Knowing** · Copper · 20–45 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Curate four exhibits from a life, an object, a person, a song, and an event, and give your partner the tour.

## First move

Each of you names four exhibits: an object, a person, a song, and an event. Give the tour in that order. The visitor asks one question per exhibit.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
