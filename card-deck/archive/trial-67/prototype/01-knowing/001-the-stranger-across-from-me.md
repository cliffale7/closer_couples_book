# 001  The Stranger Across From Me

**Knowing** · Copper · 20–45 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Interview the person you already know as if you had just met, and let a courteous question reach a fact familiarity had filed away.

## First move

One of you asks a courteous question you might ask a stranger. The other answers with one fact, not a speech. Then switch.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
