**Play** · Amber · 20–45 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Three short stories, one of them a generous fiction, and a second look at the two that really happened.

## First move

Each of you tells three short stories. One is a generous fiction. The other guesses, then you look again at the two that really happened.
