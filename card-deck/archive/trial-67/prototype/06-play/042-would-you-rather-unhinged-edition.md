# 042  Would You Rather: Unhinged Edition

**Play** · Amber · 20–45 minutes · low energy · light

## Invitation

A forced choice between two invented options, a one-sentence reason, and a third door if the question is a cage.

## First move

Offer two invented choices. They pick one and give a one-sentence reason. If the question feels like a cage, they may open a third door or pass.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
