# 056  The Questions Without Answers

**Inner** · Indigo · 20–45 minutes · low energy · deep

## Invitation

Six open questions, a blank if you need one, and a closing move that refuses the last word.

## First move

Each of you brings one question you cannot close. A blank is allowed. End without forcing a last word.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
