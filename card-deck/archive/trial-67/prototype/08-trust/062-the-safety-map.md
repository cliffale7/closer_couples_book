# 062  The Safety Map

**Trust** · Slate · 20–45 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Map the actions, situations, and words that help you open, and the ones that bring your guard up.

## First move

Each of you names two things that help you open, and two that bring your guard up. This is a map, not a verdict.
