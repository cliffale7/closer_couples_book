# 030  The Compliment You Can’t Dodge

**Chosen** · Rose · 20–45 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

One precise compliment, 10 quiet seconds, and a thank-you that stays put.

Give one precise compliment. The other person waits ten quiet seconds, then says thank you and stops. No deflecting.
