# 025  What Loving Me Actually Looks Like

**Chosen** · Rose · 20–45 minutes · low energy · personal

## Invitation

Two lists of visible acts, read side by side, with no type and no score.

## First move

Each of you writes five visible acts that feel like love to you. Read them side by side. Do not score which list is better.

## On the card

Passing is a finished turn.
This card does not ask for touch. A yes to the exercise is not consent to touch or sex.
