## The hook

The title sounds like a cape. The exercise is closer to a recent Tuesday. You each look at one less flattering tendency you can already see in yourself: jealousy, vanity, resentment, control, scorekeeping, withdrawal, or a cousin of those. You keep it specific. You keep it yours. You use a small recent example, the kind that still fits in one scene, and you say what you think the tendency buys you.

Your partner's job is to show they heard you. They may say how it lands. They do not prosecute an older file, and they do not rush to erase the thing you are trying to see. This is self-awareness at a size you can survive. It is a poor arena for a roast, a diagnosis, or a contest over who is worse.

## Why it might matter

An unflattering habit described by its owner, at the size of one example, can be looked at. The same habit described by a partner, or inflated to a lifetime, becomes a case. Cases produce defense. Defense is how a tendency keeps its job.

The mechanism is ownership plus proportion. You say what you do, what you think it protects or purchases, the effect you are willing to name, and one thing you are not asking your partner to manage. Hearing yourself say the purchase price is different from being accused of it. Hearing your partner repeat it back, without a pardon and without a prosecution, is different from being reassured that you are fine.

Self-awareness includes scale. A glass left in the sink can reveal scorekeeping. It cannot support a theory of your entire character. Proportion is part of honesty.

## Before you begin

Lay the cards face up. Each of you will choose one you can discuss without drowning. You may decline any card. A declined card leaves the table, and the decline is a full turn. You may choose another, or you may say, "Nothing from this menu tonight." If both of you say that, the session is complete.

Bring a tendency you can look at, tonight, at the size of a recent example. If what you are actually looking at is coercion, sexual pressure, or monitoring of a partner's movements and messages, stop this game. Say the plain thing in a direct conversation. When there is danger, outside help belongs in that plan. Jealousy and control, on this menu, mean the smaller social kinds: a hot feeling about attention, a grip on plans and clocks. Possessiveness is not treated here as proof of love, and it is not a style to admire.

Either of you may refuse a tendency you do not want in the air. Stop if either of you is flooded. "Pause" ends the scene. No eye contact is required. Letters are a full version.

The listener is in the example sometimes. The listener is not cast as the cause, and is not assigned the repair. Harmful behavior remains harmful after it has been described. Receptive listening is the style of this hour. It is not a pardon.

You do not owe a childhood, a diagnosis, or the worst night of your life. Clinical nicknames stay off the table. Ordinary words are the whole vocabulary.

## The experience

Choose who goes first by a coin, or by who is already clear. One tendency each. A second tendency in the same night slides toward a contest. Leave it for another sitting unless both of you, after the first round, explicitly want one more. Default is one.

### The four beats

The speaker uses a recent small example. They speak in the first person. If the sentence trying to get out is "you make me this way," they pause and return to what they themselves do.

1. **What I do.** The example, plainly, with the tedious details left in if they keep it small.
2. **What it buys.** What you think the tendency protects or purchases for you. Status, safety, a private sense of fairness, an image, a way to avoid surprise.
3. **The effect I am willing to name.** One effect, at the size of the example. You choose the size. Your partner does not enlarge it.
4. **What I am not handing you.** One thing you are not asking your partner to manage. A chart, a vow, a new way of praising you, a promise to stop teasing, the job of noticing the tendency before you do. If you do want a specific response, it belongs in the optional sentence at the end, and it is a request, not a sentence they must serve.

Then stop. The speaker does not add a worse example to prove sincerity.

### The listener

1. Reflect what you heard in two sentences at most. Stay with the four beats.
2. You may say how it lands, in one sentence, without opening an older incident.
3. If they are trying to see clearly, leave the eraser in the drawer. "You are not really like that" can be kind, and it can also steal the look they just took. You may say what you appreciate. Appreciation can sit beside the tendency. It does not have to delete it.
4. Do not expand the confession, diagnose, score who is worse, or begin your own turn inside theirs.
5. If you are flooded, say "pause."

Matching them with a worse story of your own is a later turn, about you, if you still want a turn. It is not comfort, and it is not a duet.

### The optional sentence

When both turns are done, each speaker may say: "When this shows up, a useful response from me is ___. A useful response from you is ___."

Either blank may stay blank. A blank is information. It means you are not outsourcing the watch, or you do not yet know what help would actually help.

If you have no watch-for yet, say that. "I do not have a signal yet" completes the session. Reform is not the exit ticket. A clearer look is.

### The menu

Choose with the cards in view. Read the steer before you commit. If a card's example would have to be the worst one you own, choose a different card or pass.

```cards
Jealousy | A recent small moment when you wanted attention, ease, or status someone else had, and what you did with that want. Keep possessiveness at that size. If the pattern is surveillance or a threat, leave the game.
Vanity | A recent moment when you needed the flattering version of yourself to be the one on record.
Resentment | One recent item on a tally you have not said out loud. Leave the archive in the drawer.
Control | A recent time you steered a plan, a tone, or a clock so you would not have to be surprised. Monitoring a phone, a body, or a person's whereabouts is outside this card.
Scorekeeping | A recent count of who did the last chore, kindness, or repair, and the satisfaction or the sting inside the count.
Withdrawal | A recent time you left the conversation without leaving the building: silence, a screen, a delay.
Envy | Wanting a life, a skill, or a freedom that is not yours to take, as it showed up once lately.
Image management | A time you were agreeable so the picture of you would stay pleasant, separate from whether you agreed.
Stubbornness | A recent moment when you already saw the other point and kept arguing.
Competitiveness | A recent time you needed to win against someone you love, over something that did not need a winner.
Dismissal | One joke or shrug that made another person's feeling smaller.
Martyrdom | An extra thing you did, and the part of you that needed it noticed.
Suspicion | A motive you drafted for someone before you asked them. One recent draft.
Your own words | Name a cousin of these in ordinary language. No clinical nickname. One recent example, or pass.
```

## Three ways to play

**15 minutes.** One tendency in the room. Three sentences that cover the example, what it buys, and one signal you hope to catch sooner. The listener reflects once. The other person may pass. A pass completes their half. This is the short path: one tendency, one example, one hoped-for signal.

**40 minutes.** Both of you, one card each, all four beats, a real reflection, and the optional sentence with permission for blanks. This is the version that lets the purchase price get said out loud, which is usually the part people skip in favor of apology or accusation.

**70 minutes.** Write the four beats as a short letter first. Exchange them. Read in silence. Then speak, and let the listener's reflection meet the letter rather than a performance. Still one tendency each unless you both ask for a second. The extra time is for accuracy and for stopping if someone is flooded. Letters travel. Eye contact remains optional.

## Going deeper

On a later day, each of you may report one sighting of your own tendency, in a single sentence, without a trial. You report yourself. A sighting of your partner is not your report. If you want to talk about something they did, that is a different conversation, asked for in plain language, outside this chapter's shape.

You can also revisit only the optional sentence and fill a blank you left empty, or confirm that the blank should stay blank. Changed blanks are worth dating. A useful response that has become a new chore is worth retiring.

If the tendency has changed costume, say what the new costume looks like, still with one example. Costumes are how these habits stay employed. Naming the new outfit is the deeper round. A full confession of origin is not required, and it is not the prize.

## Variations and replay

By letter or by call, use the same four beats and the same limit on the listener. Apart, read the letter twice before you answer. The first reading is for your own pulse. The second is for the reflection.

Low energy: one card, one example, reflection, stop. Skip the optional sentence rather than inventing a request you do not mean.

If speech is tiring, stay on the page and let the listener write the two-sentence reflection underneath, then read it aloud or hand it back. If writing is tiring, speak the beats and let the listener jot the "what it buys" line so the wording stays yours.

Replay when you can look at the same tendency a size smaller, or when a new one is the honest choice. Date both. The later page can show whether you caught it sooner. It can also show that you did not. Both are records.

The motif is a shadow given a face and drawn without scorn. If you sketch anything, keep the face specific and the pencil ordinary. Scorn is a different instrument.

009, The Uncomfortable Truth, is one uncomfortable fact with its own shape. 013, Confession Without Consequence, is a chosen embarrassment, and the listening there still leaves harmful behavior harmful. 088, The Thing I’m Still Figuring Out, is an unresolved question you are in the middle of. Tonight is a tendency you can already describe.

## Examples

Fictional. Rowan and Vale are an illustration, not a portrait of you.

Rowan chooses Scorekeeping and stays with one glass. Last week Rowan noticed a private count of who starts the dishes, and a small hot satisfaction when Vale left a glass by the sink. What it buys, Rowan says, is a sense of fairness Rowan can hold without having to ask for anything. The effect Rowan is willing to name: the kitchen went colder than a glass requires. What Rowan is not handing Vale: a chore chart, or a new duty to praise the dishes.

Vale reflects, "You count, it buys you a private fairness, and the room gets colder than the glass." Vale does not say Rowan is not really like that. Vale does not import a fight from March. Vale adds one landing sentence: "I felt the cold, and I did not know there was a count." Then Vale stops.

Vale chooses Vanity. At a dinner last month, Vale told a story that kept Vale looking calm, and edited out having been rude to a barista that afternoon. What it buys is the seat of the person who has it together. The effect Vale will name: the story was prettier than the afternoon, and the barista remained outside the plot. Vale is not asking Rowan to stop teasing, and is not asking Rowan to advertise Vale's goodness to repair it.

Rowan reflects the sanded story and the edited barista. Rowan does not offer a second example of Vale's vanity. Rowan does not absolve it. Rowan fills one blank, "a useful response from me is to say the count out loud before the kitchen changes temperature," and leaves the response from Vale blank. Vale leaves both blanks blank and says so. The blanks stand. Nobody is crowned more honest.

## Afterward

Stay with the size you chose.

What did the tendency turn out to be buying?
Did the listener keep the example at that size?
Where did you want to erase it, enlarge it, or match it with a worse one?
Which blank are you glad stayed blank?

You can see a tendency clearly and still like the person who has it, including when that person is you. Clarity is the evening. A renovation schedule is a different project, started only if you want one.

## Notes

Reimagined from the broad tradition of moral inventories and character examinations. No particular school's map of the psyche is adopted here, and none was used as a source. No empirical claim is made. No whitelist ID is claimed, and none is invented. The activity is a bounded conversation: one owned tendency, one recent example, four beats, a listener who reflects and stops. It is the wrong container for coercion, sexual pressure, or monitoring of a partner's movements and messages. Those need a direct conversation and, when there is danger, outside help. Describing a tendency does not pardon harm. Possessiveness is not offered as romance. A traumatic origin is not required. The cruelest available sentence is not the goal. Proportion is part of the exercise.
