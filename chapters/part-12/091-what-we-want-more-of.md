## The hook

A lot of couple talk is a list of removals. Less snapping. Less phone. Less of that joke. Removals matter, and they are a different conversation. This one starts from appetite. More walks. More of the stupid joke that is actually funny. More quiet in the same room. More of a kindness you already know how to do on your good days.

What We Want More Of asks each of you for five presences, then a rewrite of any line that is secretly an accusation. Together you pick one shared increase and one individual increase, for a short, stated period. The individual one does not need the other person's labor. Rest and maintenance count. A good life is allowed to want more of what already works, and it is also allowed to stay at enough.

## Why it might matter

Complaints are easy to hear as indictments, even when the wish inside them is simple. Starting from appetite keeps the wish in its own clothes. "More of the slow kissing in the kitchen, before anyone decides anything else" can be received, questioned, or declined as a wish. "You never touch me" cannot be tried on a Thursday. It can only be defended.

Specific wishes are easier to meet with curiosity. There is research nearby about telling someone a good event, and about responses that build on the telling. That work is about sharing news that already happened. It is not a test of scheduling more of a behavior, and this list is not that study. The reminder, for us, is only that a concrete good thing can be heard. The hearing in this chapter is manners you choose. It is not a research protocol.

063, The Appreciation Behind the Complaint, starts from an irritation and looks for the wish under it. This chapter starts before the complaint, at the appetite itself. 106, The Intimacy Negotiators, has its own way of weighing wants. Leave your five items here. They are not points in that other game.

## Before you begin

Include items you can do at home, at low cost, and on low-energy days. Travel, restaurants, and late nights can appear. They cannot be the whole list. Sensory limits and pain shape what "more" can mean. A wish that costs the other person's body, sleep, or other relationships can be said, and it can be declined. More of something never overrides a limit.

You may keep one item private. Sex, time, money, and family are information, not entitlements. If a line is really about an ongoing harm, park it for a direct conversation. This chapter does not repair a wound by asking for extra dessert.

Passing is allowed. So is a list of three honest items when five would be padding.

## The experience

10 minutes of writing. Each person lists five "more of" items that name a presence. Shared walks. A kind of joke. A sexual or affectionate pattern, if you want that on the page. Quiet in the same room. Time with friends. Making something. A weekday ritual. Letters, overlapping calls, a shared watch-list if you live apart. Maintenance: more of going to bed before you are ruined. More of an ordinary Tuesday that does not improve itself into an event.

Read the list to yourself before you read it aloud. Any item that is secretly "less of your flaw" gets rewritten until the positive action is visible. "Less of you being distracted" might become "more of 10 minutes with the phone in the other room, if you want that too." If you cannot rewrite it without blaming, park it. Parked lines are for a direct request outside this chapter. They do not get smuggled back in with prettier verbs.

Share the lists. Each person asks one curious question, aimed at texture. "When you say more walks, do you mean talking walks or quiet ones?" "Is the drawing hour something you want witnessed, or protected?" One question. Then stop interviewing.

Together, choose one shared increase and one individual increase to try for a short, stated period. Two weeks is a humane default. The shared increase is something you will both invite, and either of you can still decline a given day. The individual increase belongs to one person. The other may cheer, stay out of the way, or join if asked. They are not assigned the labor.

Write the two chosen increases, the period, and a date to notice what happened. The notice is a description. It is not an audit of compliance. "We walked once, and the second week it became standing at the window in coats" is a successful review. A quota is how appetite turns into debt.

## Three ways to play

**15 minutes.** Three items each. Rewrite anything that blames. Pick one increase, shared or individual, and a date to notice it. Say the parked lines only if you need to mark that they are parked. A short list with one real try is a finished session.

**35 minutes.** Five items, the rewrite, one question each, one shared increase, one individual increase, a period, and a noticing date. This is the version that protects the wish that does not require a partner.

**55 minutes.** After the choices, each person describes the smallest version of their increase on a bad day, and the version that would be too much. Write those beside the items. The extra minutes keep "more" from meaning "unlimited." Still no scores.

## Going deeper

A month later, tell each other what increased, including the increases that happened sideways. Sometimes the walk did not happen and the joke did. Keep the appetite that is still hungry, and retire the one that turned out to be a mood from a single afternoon.

You can sort the list, on a second pass, into "together," "beside each other," and "mine alone." Beside each other is a respectable category: reading in the same room, working at the same table, napping within earshot. It is easy to forget, and it is often what people mean by more closeness when they are tired of plans.

If "more sex" or "more talk" appears, give it texture before it leaves the page. Which hour, which pace, which kind of talk, which kind of touch, and what a no looks like in the middle. A textured wish can still be declined. An untextured wish is a fog the other person cannot consent to.

## Variations and replay

Across distance, more of letters, a standing call, or a show you watch in the same hour counts. The individual increase might be a local class the other person will never see. Witnessing it can be a photo or a sentence, if the dreamer wants a witness.

The motif is a cup with more in it, sitting on an ordinary table: `assets/chapters/091/motif.svg`. No rising chart. Enough is a legal result.

032, The Everyday Devotion Experiment, picks a practice of care and lives with it. 063 begins at the complaint. Come back when the period ends, or when a new appetite shows up and you want it named before it becomes a grievance. Date the noticing. What increased, and what you still want, can both be true.

## Examples

Fictional. Rowan and Vale are an illustration, not a portrait of you.

Rowan's first draft includes "more help with the week" and "more sex." The help line, read twice, is a complaint about Vale's chores. Rowan parks it for a direct request on Sunday and does not pretend the park is a wish. The sex line gets texture: "More of the slow kissing in the kitchen, before anyone decides whether the night goes further. Either of us can stop at the kissing."

The rest of Rowan's list: more of reading in the same room, more of the ridiculous voice Vale uses for the cat, more of a drawing hour that does not need an audience, more of leaving a party while they still like the party.

Vale's list: more of soup on cold Sundays, more of overlapping phone-free breakfast, more of seeing friends without turning it into a reunion, more of being asked before plans land on the calendar, more of naps.

Vale asks whether the kitchen kissing is a hope or a standing expectation. Rowan says hope, and a no is a complete sentence. Rowan asks whether the naps are social. Vale says the naps are alone, and company means not being teased for them.

Shared increase, for two weeks: reading in the same room on two evenings, which can shrink to 15 minutes. Individual increase: Rowan draws for half an hour on Wednesday, door shut, and Vale does not turn it into a critique session. Noticing date, the Sunday after next. They agree the chore conversation will happen, and that it will not wear this list as a costume.

## Afterward

Which line was an appetite, and which line was a flaw in a nicer shirt?
What did you each ask, and what did you learn about texture?
Which increase needs nobody's permission but your own?
Where did a wish bump into a limit, and was the limit treated as real?

You can want more of a life that is already good. You can also hear a more, love the person who said it, and keep your no.

## Our Story

Date the session. Note the version, the place, and whether you finished or paused. Initials and mood are optional. Write the two chosen increases and the date you will notice them.

The two personal pages are yours. Share them, keep them, or leave them blank. Unread is a complete way to use them. One private list item can stay off the shared page. The shared notes are the part you write together.

```journal
session: date, time, place, version played, mood if wanted, intention, initials if wanted, done or paused
partner_a:
- An experience or action you want more of, named as an appetite rather than as a problem to solve.
- The shared increase or the individual one you are trying, and for how long.
partner_b:
- What you want more of, from your own appetite, which may not match.
- An item you rewrote until the presence was visible, or one you parked.
shared:
- The overlap you will actually invite in, and the individual wishes you will protect.
- What actually happened
- The line or moment worth keeping
- What we discovered
- What we might do next, if anything
revisit:
- What increased, and what you still want more of.
- Attempt, date, and what changed
- Then versus now
```

## Notes

See G2004. Telling someone about a good event was associated with greater positive affect and well-being beyond the event itself. Perceived active-constructive responses were associated with higher relationship quality; passive or destructive responses were not the helpful pattern. G2004 does not study a list of behaviors to increase, and it is not evidence that this chapter changes a relationship. The wish here is specific so it can be heard. How you hear it is this book's manners, not a definition borrowed from that paper. A rising line is not required. Rest and maintenance count as more of. Wishes are information, and limits still hold.
