## The hook

End on the quality that survives the costume. Move the two of you: a different century, a delayed train, a job neither of you has, a city you have only passed through. Keep your actual faces. The costume can be ridiculous. The question underneath is strict.

Imagine one meeting in a different era or circumstance. Once the hat, the trade, and the accident of timing come off, what is still this person, and would it still draw you? If the scene never arrives at that question, you have been to a costume party. Charming, and not this chapter. 046, The Alternate Universe Couple, ends on one trait carried back from a shared plot. This chapter does not found a caper.

## Why it might matter

The story of how you met is sticky. The bar, the app, the friend, the year the city felt a certain way. Attraction gets braided into that lighting until the person and the set are hard to separate. A changed meeting lifts the set. What remains is recognition, or a blank, and a blank is allowed.

"What if we had met another way" is a familiar romantic game. Treat it as imaginative play with a precise last question. It is not a clinical assessment, a past-life exercise, or a history exam. Nothing on this project's shelf shows that a counterfactual conversation increases desire or reveals a trait called chemistry, fate, or soulmates. The present relationship is not on trial. If the ferry is more charming than Tuesday, Tuesday is still the life you have. The chapter fails when the fantasy is used to call the real person a disappointment.

The Alternate Universe Couple invents other versions of you and follows their misadventures as a story. Keep that door shut. This chapter keeps the real persons and asks what would survive one changed meeting. The Moment I Knew, The Attraction Interview, and The Life Not Taken belong to other nights.

## Before you begin

Each person chooses one meeting condition. An era is allowed. So is a circumstance with no costume: a delayed train, a different trade, a festival, a city neither of you has lived in, a night shift, a flooded bookstore, a ferry, a language class, an animal shelter after hours. You do not need a bibliography. If someone starts scoring historical accuracy, they are at the wrong party.

Either of you may discard a door. Keep circumstance prompts beside eras so nobody has to role-play a time that carries family pain, political pain, or bodily pain. If a scene only works by making one of you smaller under the law, pick another door. An era of legal inequality was not a simpler or more passionate world. The question is which quality belongs to the person in the room.

The imagined meeting is not a request to act a sex scene or to adopt period gender roles. Flirtation inside the fiction can be specific and adult. When the scene ends, nobody owes the kiss or the undressing to the actual evening. Skip the stock picture of a woman and a man in formal clothes. A research ship, a print shop, a night market, or two people covered in flour will do.

Talk or write. Long-distance fits. There is no mobility demand. Fatigue means one condition each and shorter sentences, which is already the shape. Either of you may pass and suggest a walk.

## The experience

Use the standard version when you have about 40 minutes. The short version is the same skeleton without the private writing.

1. Apart, choose the condition. In the standard sitting, take about 8 minutes and write five lines: where, when, what each of you is doing, one sensory detail, and the moment you notice the other person. You are both yourselves. Clothes may change. Personalities may not.
2. Tell the scene in three beats, in this order, so the costume cannot eat the evening.
   - How you notice each other. A competence, a tenderness, a shameless interest in the food, or a body. "I would still want your hands" is a legal sentence.
   - Which quality is still this person, in language you could use on a Tuesday. "You still interrupt yourself when you get excited." "You still notice who has not been offered food." If the quality is stuck to the hat, take the hat off and try again.
   - One thing that other life would have hidden, which you are glad you know now. Illness, a friendship, a sexual truth, a grief, the silence after work. This beat keeps the game from becoming nostalgia for a life that never paid a bill.
3. If beat three becomes a complaint that real life is the dull version, return to beat two. The other meeting can be gorgeous, and you can still be glad you know the person who forgets lunch.
4. The listener has two sentences and no critique. "The quality I heard was ______. One quality I would still notice, even in that meeting, is ______." Add a recognition. Do not correct their history.
5. Switch. The second scene does not have to rhyme with the first. Comedy is welcome. Meanness is not.
6. Close with one line each. "What would still attract me is ______." If you cannot fill it, say so. An honest blank is a result. You do not repair it before you leave.

See each other, then get out. A plot, a villain, or a second decade is how people avoid the question. Love a detail, and still refuse to live in the costume for the whole sitting.

## Three ways to play

### Short, 20 minutes

No writing. Take 3 minutes, total, to choose one condition each and discard any door that hurts. Give the first person 6 minutes for the three beats and the listener 2 minutes. Reverse that 8-minute block. Leave 1 minute for the closing lines. Short sentences still count. Use this for fatigue, a late night, or a bad connection.

### Standard, 40 minutes

Use 4 minutes to agree that the real persons stay, and that the present relationship is not being graded. Write in private for 8 minutes. Give each scene about 10 minutes, including the listener's two sentences. Leave 8 minutes for the closing lines and a little quiet, in case someone wants to scrap a scene and use the train instead. A discard is in time if it happens before you have spent the whole 10 minutes defending the first idea.

### Why there is no extended version

A second era, a plot, and invented selves belong to The Alternate Universe Couple. Stretching the costume until the surviving-attractor question never arrives is the failure this design prevents. One meeting each is the chapter. If you are tired, shorten the sentences. Do not add doors.

## Going deeper

Name the kind of attraction. Sexual, aesthetic, intellectual, moral, comic, or two of those on one ferry. "I would still want your mouth when you are concentrating" and "I would still trust you with a frightened stranger" can share a scene. A vague "you have a vibe" fails because a Tuesday cannot recognize it.

The hidden thing you are glad to know is where you choose a person rather than a poster. The other life might have kept the charm and hidden the illness, the debt, the kink, the temper, the faith, the way grief sits in the house. Gladness does not mean the hard thing is easy. It means attraction is allowed to include reality. If you are not glad, skip the performance of gladness. Say what was hidden, and say you are still learning what to do with knowing it.

Listener, add a quality you have actually seen. "I would still notice that you track the exits and the feelings at the same time" is anchored. A timeless soul is fog. If the scene stirs a real wish, let it stand as a wish. On another day you can take it to The Desire Nobody Asked About, where hearing it will not be a yes. You can also let it evaporate. The time machine does not issue tickets.

## Variations and replay

Write the five lines as a short letter and read them on a call. The listener still gets two sentences before you switch.

Make circumstance cards: train, night shift, bookstore flood, election-night kitchen, ferry, hospital cafeteria as visitors, animal shelter, language class, fair, research ship. Draw one. If either of you hates it, discard and draw again.

Replay with a new door. If you want the same ferry again because it was fun, say you are repeating it for pleasure, and still ask the three beats. A do-over until the attraction sounds impressive is a different game.

## Examples

Rowan and Vale are fictional. They are not the two of you.

Rowan starts in a 1920s newsroom and scraps it almost at once. The scene wants Vale rescued, plus a quip about the hat, plus praise for a "simpler" era. New door: a night ferry between two cities neither of them has lived in, both of them covering someone else's shift. Rowan notices Vale giving the good blanket to a stranger and then looking embarrassed. The surviving quality is that Vale still hides a tenderness and gets shy about it. The ferry would have hidden Vale's ordinary silence after work, and Rowan is glad to know it, because that silence is how Vale comes back into a room. Vale answers, "The quality I heard was the hidden kindness. One quality I would still notice is that you track the exits and the feelings at the same time."

Vale's scene is an agricultural fair. Both of them are publicly bad at a pie. The noticing is sexual and comic at once: flour on Rowan's wrist, and the way Rowan loses without getting cruel. The surviving quality is competence failing in public while the person stays decent. The thing a pie contest would have hidden is Rowan's specific way of wanting, which Vale names in one plain sentence and does not ask to act out. Rowan reflects the decency, then adds a quality: "I would still notice that you narrate your own embarrassment before anyone else can use it."

They close. Rowan: "What would still attract me is the kindness you try to smuggle." Vale: "What would still attract me is the way you want, which I only know because this is the life we have." They do not grade the apartment against the ferry. They do the dishes that are actually in the sink.

## Afterward

You can kiss if you both want a kiss. You can also make tea. The scene is not a request, and the more cinematic meeting does not get a vote on your evening.

If one of you feels a pang that real life is less scored with string quartets, say the pang in one sentence and then name the Tuesday quality again. The pang is human. Letting it steer the rest of the night turns the game into a complaint. You are allowed to prefer your actual person and still enjoy a ferry.

## Our Story

Write while the scene is still warm, or the next morning. Private pages are optional to share. The person who wrote one decides. Pass a prompt, change it, or leave it blank.
