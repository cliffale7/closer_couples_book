## 3. RESEARCH PROTOCOL: SERIOUS THINKING, NOT PSEUDO-SCIENCE

Research BEFORE writing whenever it can improve the experience. Use legitimate publicly accessible sources, libraries, academic databases, original journal papers when possible, books with dependable bibliographic data, and established relationship practitioners; seek primary sources for eye gazing, self-disclosure, responsiveness, listening, gratitude, novelty, affectionate touch, relationship maintenance, and conflict repair. Cross-check secondary summaries. Examples of relevant areas include Arthur Aron's interpersonal closeness research and reciprocal self-disclosure, Gottman-style love maps, active-constructive responding, perceived partner responsiveness, self-expansion, and relationship gratitude; investigate what each source actually supports rather than repeating popular simplifications.

Each chapter's research packet should distinguish:

- **Empirical evidence:** What particular experiment actually measured, with participants/context and limitations.
- **Clinical/practitioner framework:** Useful perspectives, not necessarily results from controlled studies.
- **Common tradition / established game:** Widely practiced but perhaps without meaningful clinical evidence.
- **Original creative design:** Reasoned hypothesis and experience design, not a scientifically proven method.

Classify each entry as `established`, `reimagined`, `original-formulation`, or `mixed`, explaining the choice. Mark 97–108 as **created for this project / original formulations**, without making exclusivity claims. Be especially precise about the popular 36-questions activity and eye-gazing claims: do not suggest that four minutes of eye contact automatically induces love. Note discomfort, cultural variation, individual differences, or sensory constraints as relevant, without drowning the book in disclaimers.

Build `research/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`, `research/SOURCE_LEDGER.csv` and a claims-to-sources ledger. Use actual verified DOI/URL/author/date for every empirical citation. If browsing is unavailable, state which references are unverified in a dedicated research status file and remove unsupported empirical assertions from book copy; do not fabricate citations. Include tasteful endnotes/selected reading rather than citations overwhelming activity prose. Respect copyright; paraphrase thoughtfully, credit adaptations, and avoid copying large preexisting questionnaires verbatim without permissions.

## 4. BOOK'S EDITORIAL PERSONALITY, AUDIENCE, AND ETHOS

The readers are **mature, thoughtful, fun, open-minded adults**, particularly a couple who already enjoy one another and want a richer connection. The prose can be witty, warm, occasionally mischievous, candid, intimate, irreverent where earned, philosophical, and sexy where appropriate. The voice is that of an intelligent, entertaining human guide—not a therapist, school counselor, social media influencer, or motivational-poster generator. Write to **both participants**, not as though one is the patient or the other the healer. Avoid stereotypes and gender-role assumptions. Avoid mandatory scoring of relationship quality or false promises that every exercise causes measurable change.

Do not turn every chapter into formulaic question lists. If the concept calls for physical presence, creative writing, movement, listening, roleplay, narrative, improvisation, cooperative creation, music, intimacy, or a multi-day mission, design the mechanism fully. Some chapters should be hysterically funny; some poignant; some gently uncomfortable; some romantic; some contemplative. Enrich the book with original illustrative examples, not invented personal anecdotes about the couple.

Treat the relationship as something worth celebrating and exploring, rather than something requiring repair. The book is distinct from the scavenger-hunt concept and must NEVER mention, incorporate, or design around that separate project.

For honest disclosure: clearly distinguish voluntary private thoughts from revelations involving another person's safety or consequential choices. A game labeled "Confession Without Consequence" cannot promise freedom from real-world consequences; explain that it establishes a receptive discussion style, not immunity. Every activity permits passing, pausing, changing a question, or modifying a touch-based component. Consent is specific and ongoing; a game choice does not imply consent to physical or sexual behavior. Keep these principles elegantly woven into the reader-facing text; don't overwhelm every page with repetition.

### Chapter length / substance

Target **approximately 1,200–2,200 meaningful words of activity prose** for most of the 108 entries, with longer chapters for intricate games or signature sessions. Do not pad simple concepts to meet a quota; expand them with genuinely useful sample prompts, mini-rounds, troubleshooting, alternatives, related insights, and careful debriefs. A truly simpler entry can be shorter if its full experience still receives a satisfying, well-developed treatment; log reasons for exceptions. Chapters may span many pages because the memoir section adds additional writable space. The manuscript should be a genuinely substantial tome, not just 108 two-paragraph stubs.

## 5. MANDATORY CHAPTER TEMPLATE — EVERY ONE OF THE 108

Each entry must have the following meaningful elements, adjusted in voice, length, and sequence to fit the activity:

1. **Stable ID and exact original title**, part, evocative subtitle/deck, and a short invitation designed to make two people actually want to begin.
2. **At a glance:** primary estimated time + short/standard/extended paths where sensible; emotional intensity; energy; format; location; equipment/materials; setup effort; relevant mood tags; whether writing, affection, or discussion is involved; if repeatable; optional preparations.
3. **The hook:** an arresting opening, vignette, question, humorous observation, compelling explanation, or situation designed uniquely for THIS chapter.
4. **Why it might matter:** a substantial but readable explanation of the psychological/relationship mechanism or compelling experiential theory, honest about level of research evidence, not generic claims about "strengthening your bond."
5. **Before you begin:** precise space, atmosphere, materials, accessibility alternatives, agreed boundaries, consent considerations where applicable, and suggested roles.
6. **The complete guided experience:** clear sequential setup, rounds, timing, specific prompts, how to take turns, examples, how to handle silence or unexpected emotions, what counts as completing a game, and possible paths. A person should be able to perform the exercise without inventing missing rules.
7. **Short, standard, and extended editions** when plausible: e.g. 5–10 minute micro version; regular 20–45 minute version; 60–90+ minute deep edition. Do not force the same length options onto all games; use real mechanics appropriate to each.
8. **Going deeper:** at least several worthwhile optional escalations in intellectual/emotional intimacy or playful complexity. Always keep voluntary participation.
9. **Variations and replay value:** different moods, indoors/outdoors, low energy, budget, mobility, long-distance adaptation if useful, randomizer rules, multiple prompt banks, or revisit twists; avoid perfunctory variations.
10. **Examples and sample content:** conversations, mini-scripts, decks, lists, dilemmas, roleplay scenarios, fictional example answers, or filled examples to remove ambiguity. Explicitly label all examples fictional.
11. **Afterward / debrief:** nuanced personal and shared questions, including what surprised us, what was easy or difficult, what we learned, and what—if anything—we want to carry into life.
12. **Our Story journal module:** structured personal and shared writing areas tied to THIS specific exercise; see detailed memoir system below. Include space for a dated first session and a method to record repeated sessions.
13. **References / author's notes:** citations, origin/provenance classification, adaptation acknowledgment if needed, or a simple research-context note, supplied unobtrusively.
14. **Original visual treatment:** unique chapter motif and appropriate diagram/card/worksheet/illustration(s), with a consistent book-wide style.
15. **Cross-links:** suggest 2–4 relevant other experiences by verified ID, with functional links in digital editions; no invented numbers.

**Hard rule:** No chapter may consist solely of a title, a research blurb, a list of questions, and a blank worksheet. The complete activity needs a coherent experience with a beginning, middle, and satisfying close.

## 6. THE LIVING MEMOIR — ESSENTIAL NEW REQUIREMENT

The book is simultaneously a guide and a lasting archive of a couple's shared life. The journal is not a token Notes page tacked onto the back. Architect it as a FIRST-CLASS PART of every experience, with both in-book space and a separate extensible journal system.

### Every activity must offer:

**First-session record** with date/time/location, activity version, current mood (optional), initial intention or curiosity, participants' names or initials (optional), and a simple completion marker. Let readers choose not to quantify their feelings.

**Two individual voices, equal dignity:**
- "My experience" — space for Participant A to write privately before choosing what to share.
- "My experience" — parallel space for Participant B; fair, equal allotments and similarly thoughtful prompts.
- Distinct prompts that suit the exact activity: an eye-gazing exercise asks about silence and reactions; a trivia game asks about surprising guesses; a vulnerability conversation asks about what felt safe, not demanding secret content be disclosed in print; a collaborative adventure asks about roles, discoveries, mishaps, and the story of the day.
- Separate private pages should be detachable or keepable separately in personal exports; never imply they must be shared or read by the other person.

**Our shared page:**
- What actually happened (narrative, not just checkboxes).
- The funniest or most unexpected moment.
- A quote, phrase, inside joke, or line worth remembering.
- What we discovered about one another.
- What we appreciated or want to remember.
- Photos, ticket stubs, drawings, sketches, or other mementos: defined physical/digital placement, caption/date, rights/privacy handling.
- A tiny optional "what next?" entry: repeat, vary, revisit, or new idea inspired by this.

**Follow-up/revisit record:**
- Attempt number, date, version, how answers or interpretations changed, what feels different from the last time, new memory, and what we'd like to try next.
- Provide a generous repeatable template, not an artificial limit of two attempts. Offer a personal "then vs now" spread for meaningful long-term comparisons.
- Index entries by activity number and date, including room for an open-ended narrative of the relationship's growth.

**Special formats:**
- Games: score sheets only if scoring is part of the fun; record surprising answers, reasons, forfeits (never coercive), house rules, and winning moments.
- Writing exercises: dedicated writing surfaces and optional letters/diary pages.
- Time-based/multi-day rituals: trackers with dates, observations, narrative check-ins, summary and revisit prompts; avoid turning affection into a compulsory productivity chart.
- Sensual experiences: optional private discreet documentation, boundaries/preferences/preferences changed, not explicit disclosure demands.
- Deep vulnerability: meaningful reflections without requiring the literal transcription of confidential confessions.
- Physical activities: optional maps/diagrams/photo spaces when appropriate.

### Physical + digital memoir delivery

1. Integrate **at least one distinct, visibly usable journal spread or generous note area** after each chapter, adjusted to chapter complexity. For longer experiences, include multiple writable pages. Think handwriting-sized line spacing, real margins, and useful prompts, not tiny lines for ten words.
2. Build `journal/REPEATABLE_SESSION_PAGES` in editable source, printable PDF, and editable DOCX. There should be unlimited reusable blanks for revisiting an activity without needing to buy/print the entire book.
3. Build a **separate companion, "Our Story" journal / memoir edition**, with elegant front matter, journal instructions, all 108 activity IDs and cross-references, repeat-session spreads, chronology index, milestone pages, memento placements, and a substantial set of adaptable blank pages. This can be as large as needed and should be distinctly marked as a companion to the main volume.
4. Provide a clear optional **Personal Keepsake edition** concept: the couple can gradually enter real notes in their own copy and regenerate a private PDF/DOCX in chronological or thematic order. Implement a documented template/data format if practical, but DO NOT manufacture personal answers or publish private data.
5. Support editable text in DOCX; if feasible and verifiably working, create fillable PDF fields or structured digital forms for primary journaling. Test saving and reopening sample dummy data; avoid promising functionality that a basic PDF/EPUB reader won't actually provide. EPUB is primarily for reading; use complementary files for longer journal entries.
6. Protect personal material: `journal/private/` must be in `.gitignore`, excluded from generic distributable exports and public remote pushes. Include an explicit private-build workflow for user-supplied notes that never silently uploads them. Provide only FICTIONAL example journal entries in sample renders.
7. Add a practical mini-section about keeping the book over years: dating entries, optional periodic comparisons, revisiting changed answers, attaching physical keepsakes, and creating an annual "chapters of us" recap.

This memoir functionality is central to the assignment and must be visibly reflected in content, layout, file structures, export pipelines, and final QA.
