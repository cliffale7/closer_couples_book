# Start here

This folder is the current draft of *The Closer Project* and *Our Story*. It is the full 7 by 10 inch book, not a shortened edition. Read `EXPORT_MANIFEST.md` for sizes and checksums.

## If you want to read

Open `The_Closer_Project_Illustrated_Workbook_v1_draft.pdf`. The contents include the 108 activities, the six signature sessions, and the indexes. "How much time" lists each real version on its own shelf. A multi-day activity is not offered there as a short session.

The Word file is the same book with the drawings embedded as pictures. The EPUB is for reading. Its pictures have alt text.

## If you want to write

`Our_Story_Companion_Journal_v1_draft.pdf` has a blank page for every activity from 001 through 108, then S01 through S06. Handwriting lines are 0.36 inch apart. Private pages are optional to share.

`Our_Story_Repeatable_Session_Pack_v1.pdf` is the blank packet for a later sitting. Write the ID, including S01–S06, and the attempt number. Keep the old sheets.

A private export stays on your computer. The commands are in `journal/EXPORT_PRIVATE_MEMOIR.md` in the project, not in this public folder. Nothing in this folder is your journal.

## If you want to play from paper

`Printable_Cards_and_Worksheets_v1_draft.pdf` is the card packet. The Branching Evening cards say a room you have already entered is closed.

## If you are reviewing the drawings

`OWNER_ART_REVIEW.pdf` shows the covers, the thirteen part openers, the six signature pictures, and all 108 motifs. Thirteen drawings were corrected. Looking at the file is not approval.

## Still yours to decide

- Whether the drawings should stand.
- Whether the prose should stand.
- Whether you and your partner want to play any of it. The field scripts are in the project under `review/phase3/FIELD_TEST_SCRIPTS.md`. No real session has been filled in.

A suggested first sitting, if you want one short evening before a long one: 001 The Stranger Across From Me in the 10-minute version, then stop. If you want a signature instead, S01 The Long Table has a 45-minute version and a longer one. Either of you can pass.
