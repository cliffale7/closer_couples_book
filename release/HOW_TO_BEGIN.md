# How to begin

This folder is the blank first draft. Nothing in it is a memory of yours.

## Tonight

Open `The_Closer_Project_Illustrated_Workbook_v1_draft.pdf`. The page size is 7 by 10 inches. The Word file is the same book if you would rather edit than print. The EPUB is for reading. Long handwriting belongs in Our Story, not in the EPUB.

Read "How to use this book." Pick one time shelf you both actually have. Skip is a finished choice. A light chapter on a hard day is a real use of the book.

If you want a surprise, set the limits first: time, energy, depth, and whether touch is welcome. Then use Surprise Us. A draw outside those limits is not the plan. Draw again.

## Writing

Each chapter has lines for a session, a page for each of you, a shared page, and a revisit. Sharing either private page is optional. A written yes is not a promise, and it is not consent when you are in the room.

`Our_Story_Companion_Journal_v1_draft.pdf` is the overflow: repeats, years, tickets, and the long record. The Word companion is the same pages, editable. Print extra copies of a sheet rather than crowding the lines.

Keep real notes on your own machine. This project does not upload them.

## Games that use paper

`Printable_Cards_and_Worksheets_v1_draft.pdf` holds the cut cards and the blank worksheets. Cut on the dashed line. Each card is 3.5 inches wide. A long card grows downward. Leave the gap before the next cut.

For The Relationship Escape Room, read the shared card aloud before either packet is opened. Hours are only 6, 8, and 10. Leave that card face up. Seal the two packets. The solved grid stays in the workbook, on its own sheets, face down until you both choose to look.

A result on a card, a star on a sheet, or a number of points is not consent to touch or sex.
