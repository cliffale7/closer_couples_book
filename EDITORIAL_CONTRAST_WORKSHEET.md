## The three tests

**Rule test:** Could someone play both activities consecutively and truthfully say the core interaction was different? If not, revise mechanics.

**Memory test:** After completing two similar activities, what memorable outcome would distinguish each? If it's 'we asked each other questions' for both, sharpen the actual discovery.

**Voice test:** Openings should not default to the same clerk/coffee metaphor; exercises should feel different in atmosphere, energy, pace and humor. Use varied *clearly fictional* examples or narrative formats; never use couple-specific real private information without authorization.

## Required before/after proof

Each revised chapter logs at least one tangible change: new turn order, better source of questions, differentiated materials, alternate character of reveal, clearer stopping condition, changed artifact, or novel guided progression. A rewritten hook alone cannot close a 'duplicate mechanics' finding. Maintain stable chapter IDs, named premise, journal prompt mapping and research citations.
