## Source premise

> Make, cook, invent, or construct something and notice how you collaborate.

## Must preserve

A finished thing, however small, and a deliberate look at collaboration. Making, cooking, inventing, or constructing all qualify. The noticing is required: who leads, who assists, what happens when something goes wrong, and whether they consult or take over. A perfect object with no talk about the process misses the premise. An imperfect object with that talk completes it.

## Evidence

No empirical citation is claimed for this activity. No teamwork or cooking study was opened, and none on the project whitelist applies. Do not infer personality from who chops or who directs.

## Tradition or practitioner frame

Cooking together and making a small object are ordinary domestic customs. This chapter adds a midpoint pause and a process debrief. It is not a validated couples workshop and not a test of roles.

## Design notes

Choose a project that can end in one sitting: a meal, a paper model, a one-page comic, a repaired hem, a cushion fort, a playlist with a handmade cover card. Match tools to skill. Allergies and safety come first. At the midpoint, pause for the four noticing questions above. At the end, look at the thing, then answer what each person did when it went sideways.

The writer should include a fictional snapshot of a lopsided collaboration being noticed without insult. Failure points: a project that needs a specialist; one person directing and criticizing; dangerous tools chosen for atmosphere; silence that never reaches the debrief.

Accessibility: seated prep, no-heat assembly (a salad, a collage), large pieces if fine motor is hard, plain-language steps, large type. Vision: tactile assembly or a narrated recipe. Hearing: written steps on the counter. Pain: a short project and a chair. Long-distance: the same simple recipe or paper project on a call, compared at the end. Strong smells and loud kitchens are optional.

## Consent and harm

Knives, heat, and heights are optional. Nobody is the apprentice unless they agree. Dietary limits are planned before shopping. Either person may change roles mid-project.

Distinguish 103, The Relationship Escape Room, which uses cooperative puzzles about personal history and skills. Entry 069 produces a tangible or clearly finished creative object and studies the collaboration, not a lock, a code, or a clue. Do not merge them, and do not turn the build into a hunt for parts around the neighborhood.

## Red flags
