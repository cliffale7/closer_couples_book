## Source premise

> Use dice, coins, or cards to choose between agreed options throughout an evening.

## Must preserve

Chance selects only among options both people have already accepted. Dice, coins, or cards are the randomizer. The evening is a sequence of those choices, plus a short look at where chance was fun and where it pinched. A veto returns a result to the menu. Chance never outranks a no. The night is not a trail of clues, and it does not reveal destiny.

## Evidence

No empirical citation is claimed for this activity. Spontaneity, randomness, and “the universe decided” have no supporting study on the project whitelist.

## Tradition or practitioner frame

Choosing a restaurant or a film by coin toss is a folk custom. This chapter extends that custom across an evening and requires the menus to be built first. It is not a clinical spontaneity treatment and not a drinking game.

## Design notes

Before any roll, build three menus of four to six options each: food or drink, activity, and conversation or music. Every option is acceptable to both. Include “stay in” and “stop the game” as faces that can come up. Each person may veto one result during the night and put that choice back. Then follow the accepted results. Debrief: where the constraint felt playful, and where it pinched.

Failure points: a menu that includes something only one person can live with, then blaming the die; locations that are unsafe; dares; spending beyond an agreed cap; an all-night pile of decisions.

Accessibility: menus include seated, quiet, low-cost, and low-sensory options. Mobility and transit are filters before the roll. Long-distance: randomize a shared film, a topic, and a snack each person already has. Vision: high-contrast dice or a spoken random number. Fatigue: three decisions, not an all-nighter. Hearing: read options aloud or in text before the roll.

## Consent and harm

A rolled result can still be refused; the veto is part of the rules, and a safety no needs no veto token. Alcohol, sex, unplanned spending, and other people stay off the default menus. Adding any of them requires a separate explicit agreement, not a lucky roll.

Distinguish 047, which offers truth, dare, or discover, and 042, which poses hypothetical pairs. Entry 068 randomizes among real, pre-accepted options for one evening. Do not merge them, and do not let the die assign a dare.

## Red flags

Do not say the universe, fate, or the relationship “decided.” Do not build a checkpoint hunt. Do not require alcohol. Do not claim research on spontaneity. The randomizer is a toy, not an authority.
