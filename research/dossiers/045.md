# Dossier 045 — The Great Debate of Absolutely Nothing

## Source premise

> Argue opposite sides of entirely absurd propositions with exaggerated seriousness.

## Must preserve

The proposition must be empty of real grievance. Partners argue opposite sides, assigned by chance, with formal seriousness, about something that cannot matter: soup as a beverage, whether socks deserve opinions, whether the moon is showing off. The closing move is explicit abandonment of the position. If a “joke” proposition is a disguised complaint about the partner, the chapter has slipped into a real conflict and should stop.

## Evidence

No empirical citation is claimed for this activity. Debate games are not shown on the project whitelist to improve real disagreements. GOTTMAN’s practitioner ideas about conflict do not belong as evidence here.

## Tradition or practitioner frame

Formal debate, dinner-table absurdity, and parlour motions (“this house believes…”) are the tradition. This is not conflict coaching and not a clinical communication exercise.

## Design notes

Prepare a hat of original propositions, marked as examples. Assign sides with a coin so nobody is stuck defending a real pet peeve. Three minutes, exaggerated gravity, no examples drawn from the couple’s actual chores or history. Then applaud the more committed absurdity and have both speakers say, in plain voice, that they do not hold the position.

Print six original motions and mark them as examples. After the coin toss, give each speaker thirty seconds to note two “serious” points so the argument is not only volume. A point might be fake evidence (“a survey of spoons”), a definition, and a grave warning about the other side. Ban anecdotes from the couple’s actual week. When the timer ends, the abandonment line is spoken in an ordinary voice, then both people name one real topic they are glad was not on the card.

Failure points: a proposition about the partner’s habits, body, family, or politics; using the game to practice winning; refusing to drop the bit afterward so the joke becomes a barb.

Accessibility: speak, write, or pass notes. No standing and no podium. Long-distance works. Fatigue: one proposition. Hearing differences are handled by text. Sensory overload: skip applause and keep the room quiet; the seriousness can be vocal without being loud.

## Consent and harm

Either person may throw out a proposition that lands on a real wound. Stopping the round is a success, not a failed sense of humor.

Distinguish 059, The Gentle Disagreement, which works through a manageable real disagreement with warmth. Entry 045 is allowed to be serious in tone only because the content is nothing. Do not merge them, and do not use 045 as a warm-up that slides into an unresolved fight.

## Red flags

Do not claim the game reduces conflict or teaches persuasion that couples should use on each other. Do not sneak a real issue in under absurd wording. Do not score intellectual status.
