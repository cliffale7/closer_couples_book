# Dossier 084 — The Dark Side of Me

- Catalog ID: 084
- Part: 11 — Radical Curiosity and the Surprising Stuff
- Access date: 2026-10-14
- Classification: reimagined
- Confidence: medium

## Source premise

> Explore less flattering tendencies such as jealousy, vanity, resentment, or control with self-awareness.

## Must preserve

Self-observation of a less flattering tendency, kept specific and owned by the speaker. A roast of the partner, a diagnostic label, or a confession of harm treated as entertainment loses the premise.

## Evidence

No empirical citation is claimed for this activity.

## Tradition or practitioner frame

Moral inventories, “shadow” talk, and character examinations are religious and therapeutic traditions. None of them was opened as a source here, and none is a validated protocol for this chapter. The activity is a bounded conversation between adults, not an adoption of any school’s map of the psyche.

## Design notes

About 30–45 minutes. Each person chooses one tendency they can discuss without drowning: jealousy, vanity, resentment, control, scorekeeping, withdrawal, or a cousin of those. Use a recent small example, not the lifetime worst. Four beats: what I do; what I think it protects or buys me; the effect I am willing to name; one thing I am not asking you to manage for me. The listener reflects what they heard, may say how it lands, and does not prosecute older incidents. They also do not rush to absolve (“you’re not really like that”) if the speaker is trying to see clearly.

Optional journal line: “When this shows up, a useful response from me is ___, and a useful response from you is ___.” Either blank can stay blank.

Failure points: clinical nicknames; a partner who takes over the confession and expands it; using “honesty” to justify contempt; matching wounds in a contest.

Accessibility: seated talk or letters. Long-distance is suitable. Stop if either person is flooded. A shorter path is one tendency, one example, one hoped-for signal, in fifteen minutes. No eye contact is required.

## Consent and harm

The chapter is optional in full. Either person may refuse a tendency they do not want in the air tonight. If control, jealousy, or resentment is currently frightening, sexual coercion, or monitoring of a partner’s movements and messages, this game is the wrong container. Those situations need a direct conversation and, when there is danger, outside help. The listener is not cast as the cause of the tendency and is not assigned to fix it. Do not merge this with 009’s single uncomfortable truth or with 013’s chosen embarrassment. Those have their own limits. Nothing said here is immunity for harmful behavior.

## Red flags

Do not diagnose personality disorders. Do not romanticize possessiveness. Do not require a traumatic origin story. Do not let “dark side” become a dare to say the cruelest thing. Self-awareness includes proportion.
