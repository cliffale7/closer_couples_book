## Source premise

> Notice small actions that deposit into or withdraw from everyday trust over time.

## Must preserve

Noticing, over time, of small ordinary actions that build everyday trust and small actions that spend it. Both directions matter. There is no numeric balance, no interest rate, and no score. The bank is a metaphor for attention, not an account the couple can audit into a verdict. The seed stays mixed: the emotional bank account is a practitioner metaphor, and the multi-day noticing practice is this book’s design. Neither is a measured ledger.

## Evidence

GOTTMAN’s clinical writing popularized the “emotional bank account” alongside love maps, bids, and repair. Treat it as a practitioner metaphor. Do not invent a study, a quotation, or a divorce statistic. G2012 found that feeling appreciative and feeling appreciated were tied to responsiveness, commitment, and maintenance behaviors, and that appreciation can be visible in behavior. That is not a test of deposits, withdrawals, or any bank metaphor, so it is not evidence for this chapter. No empirical citation is claimed for a trust ledger.

## Tradition or practitioner frame

The emotional bank account is practitioner language, not a folk game and not a financial tool. This chapter borrows the deposit-and-withdrawal image and refuses the arithmetic. It is not a validated trust intervention.

## Design notes

For a few days, each person privately notes up to three deposits and three withdrawals, then stops. A deposit might be a kept word, a check-in, or a small repair. A withdrawal might be a forgotten promise, a cutting joke, or a repeated interruption. Exchange the notes and talk about patterns. Each person names one deposit they want to repeat on purpose.

Failure points: arithmetic (“you are overdrawn”); using the notes as a prosecution exhibit; counting sex, money, or chores as a moral balance; treating rest, pain, or low energy as a withdrawal; hidden monitoring of a phone or location.

Accessibility: reliable texts, practical help during pain, and sensory accommodations are legitimate deposits. Long-distance trust is often about follow-through on calls and plans. The cap of three keeps the list from becoming a dossier. Speech or a short written log both work. Say explicitly that resting is not a withdrawal.

## Consent and harm

Notes describe observable everyday trust between the two people. They are not a file of suspicions. Do not instruct secret tracking. Either person may decline to review a note that feels like an ambush. Private memoir pages are for the couple’s own record, not evidence.

Distinguish 060, which repairs one minor misstep through acknowledgment, accountability, and a fitting amend. Entry 064 watches a short run of small moments in both directions and does not require a repair station. Do not merge them.

## Red flags

No balance math, no interest, no divorce prediction. Do not shame a partner about income; “bank” stays metaphorical. Do not connect the log to a clue trail or to watching someone in secret. Do not claim G2012 validated the metaphor.
