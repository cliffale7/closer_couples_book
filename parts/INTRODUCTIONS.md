# Part introductions

## 1

Sit down as if a polite stranger were allowed one good question. This part is interviews, private museums, and the biography you have not handed over yet. The aim is a more accurate picture. It is not a verdict, and a pass ends the question.

## 2

These hours ask for one true thing and a listener who can leave it alone. A pass is a finished turn. Curious attention is not permission to keep digging, and it is not immunity for harm.

## 3

Looking, quiet, breath, touch, and listening each get their own hour. Where research is mentioned, it stays inside what that study actually did. None of these chapters is a treatment, and none of them requires the next one.

## 4

This part is about acts you can see: thanks, letters, reassurance, and the ordinary week. A compliment is not a claim on the person who receives it. Hearing that you are loved is allowed to be enough.

## 5

Desire can be named without being scheduled. Every physical step waits for a yes that is still true in the moment. A wish, a dance, and a plan are different nights, and any of them can stop at talking.

## 6

Games, dares, and ridiculous debates live here. The score is not the point, and a dare is not consent to touch or sex. If a round stops being fun, the round is over.

## 7

Beliefs, unfinished selves, and the questions that do not need an answer tonight. You are not fixing each other. You are hearing the version of a life that does not get much airtime.

## 8

Repair, disagreement, and the map of what feels safe. A complaint can become a wish without anyone being put on trial. Stopping is part of the skill.

## 9

A small amount of money, a first try, a made-up vacation, a task you have never done together. Novelty is the tool. Nobody has to be brave on command.

## 10

Eight ways to tell the story you already have, among them a beginning, a day, a song, a timeline, and a thanks. Invent nothing about a life you did not live. A fuzzy year can stay fuzzy.

## 11

Questions you have been polite about, including the ones with no clean answer. The box, the dilemma, and the fork are different games. A pass ends the question.

## 12

Plans, promises, and the next stretch of ordinary time. An if-then line names a day. It does not bind the other person, and a hope can be revised.

## 13

Original sessions with their own rules. The hours are on the table before any packet opens. Points are not a debt. Keys stay with the person who holds them.
