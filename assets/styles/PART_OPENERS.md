# Part openers — thirteen scenes

System: Night Library. Each opener is a recto page as specified in `assets/styles/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`: a midnight headband 1.85 in tall, then a parchment page, then one scene in a 5.40 by 4.40 in frame (`viewBox="0 0 540 440"`).

These are places, not enlarged chapter motifs. Draw the one-sentence brief. Keep the locked chapter subjects in `assets/ART_MAP.json` for the 2.20 in marks. Original SVG line and flat color only. No copyrighted characters, no scavenger-hunt trail, no romance-cover clinch.

Files: `assets/parts/01-opener.svg` through `assets/parts/13-opener.svg`.

Accent notes name the second color inside an ink-and-copper drawing. They do not replace the shared palette.

---

## Part 01 — The Art of Really Knowing Someone

Entries 001–008. Theme: two people across a table, still partly unread, willing to look.

Accent: copper lamp-light on midnight glass.

**Art brief:** Draw a night library table from one end, with two lamps of unequal height, two uneven stacks of closed books, and a narrow run of blank pages down the center, ink line and copper light against a midnight window.

Alt: Part opener for The Art of Really Knowing Someone: a library table with two unequal lamps, two book stacks, and blank pages running down the center.

Leave the chapter marks alone: this is not two empty chairs, not a single closed book with a ribbon, not a museum of plinths.

## Part 02 — Vulnerability and the Courage to Be Seen

Entries 009–016. Theme: being seen is a choice about how far the shade goes up.

Accent: copper cord; the room’s warmth is parchment, not a burgundy field.

**Art brief:** Draw a tall night window with the shade raised only as far as the sill, one lamp and one empty chair inside, and solid dark outside, in ink line with a thin copper shade-cord.

Alt: Part opener for Vulnerability and the Courage to Be Seen: a night window with the shade raised to the sill, a lamp, and an empty chair.

This is a window, not armor on a stand, not a lifted envelope, not a mended cup.

## Part 03 — The Science and Experience of Closeness

Entries 017–024. Theme: closeness as attention you can feel in a room, not as a laboratory.

Accent: one teal arc.

**Art brief:** Draw a kitchen table at night with two cups, an open pocket watch, and a single teal arc hanging in the air between the cups, in ink line, with no instruments and no chart.

Alt: Part opener for The Science and Experience of Closeness: two cups on a kitchen table, an open pocket watch, and an arc between the cups.

This is a kitchen, not two profiles with minute-marks, not a phone turned face down, not a breath diagram over a bench.

## Part 04 — Love, Affection, and Feeling Chosen

Entries 025–032. Theme: the ordinary proof of being chosen, a light left on and a key that fits.

Accent: copper keys, a small gold glow in the lamp.

**Art brief:** Draw a front-hall hook holding two different keys on one ring under a single lit wall lamp, the door closed, in ink line with copper keys.

Alt: Part opener for Love, Affection, and Feeling Chosen: two different keys on one hall hook beneath a lit lamp.

No hearts, no bouquet, no couple on the cover. This is a hall, not a stack of compliment notes and not a stopped clock.

## Part 05 — Desire, Attraction, and Sensual Intimacy

Entries 033–040. Theme: adult wanting, with the room still unentered and the door able to stay as it is.

Accent: burgundy drape. This is the part opener that uses burgundy as a field of cloth.

**Art brief:** Draw a hallway threshold at night with two pairs of shoes side by side, a burgundy drape half drawn, and a lamp turned low, the room beyond only a dark shape, with no people and no bed.

Alt: Part opener for Desire, Attraction, and Sensual Intimacy: shoes at a hallway threshold, a half-drawn burgundy drape, and a low lamp.

Mature and restrained. Not a clinch, not a product still life of citrus and records, not a floor plan.

## Part 06 — Playfulness, Laughter, and a Little Mischief

Entries 041–048. Theme: wit inside a serious room; mischief for grown people.

Accent: a small gold paper crown.

**Art brief:** Draw a chessboard at the edge of a library table, one knight stepped off the board onto the wood, and a paper crown on a pawn, in ink line with a gold crown and no cartoon faces.

Alt: Part opener for Playfulness, Laughter, and a Little Mischief: a chessboard with a knight off the board and a paper crown on a pawn.

Adult mischief. Not a children’s board, not three game-show doors, not a gavel, not a scavenger map.

## Part 07 — The Inner World: Dreams, Meaning, and Identity

Entries 049–056. Theme: a private cosmos shown to one other person, still unfinished.

Accent: teal arms, gold lamp.

**Art brief:** Draw a desk orrery whose four planets are a stair, a seed, a closed eye, and a lamp, turning above an open blank journal, in ink line with teal arms and a gold lamp.

Alt: Part opener for The Inner World: an orrery of a stair, a seed, a closed eye, and a lamp above a blank journal.

Not a question-mark constellation, not a mythic beast, not a pencil portrait.

## Part 08 — Trust, Communication, and Emotional Safety

Entries 057–064. Theme: a dry place in bad weather, offered rather than argued.

Accent: teal rain, copper lantern.

**Art brief:** Draw a narrow covered porch in the rain, the house door standing open, two mugs under the eave, and a copper lantern on the dry boards between them, in ink line with teal rain.

Alt: Part opener for Trust, Communication, and Emotional Safety: a covered porch in the rain, an open door, two mugs, and a lantern.

## Part 09 — Shared Adventures and New Experiences

Entries 065–072. Theme: leaving together, with the destination still being named.

Accent: charcoal dusk sky, copper tickets.

**Art brief:** Draw a dusk train platform with two tickets in one hand, one shared suitcase, and a destination board whose place-name is only half written, in ink line with a charcoal sky and copper tickets.

Alt: Part opener for Shared Adventures and New Experiences: a dusk platform, two tickets, one suitcase, and a half-written destination board.

A departure, not a clue trail, not a marked treasure map, not dice, not two hands on a door handle.

## Part 10 — The Past, the Present, and the Story of Us

Entries 073–080. Theme: what already happened, what is still warm, and the page not yet written.

Accent: a thin gold edge on the photograph.

**Art brief:** Draw a windowsill holding a faded photograph turned toward the glass, a cup still showing a ring of heat, and a blank page with the pen set beside it, in ink line with a thin gold edge on the photograph.

Alt: Part opener for The Past, the Present, and the Story of Us: a windowsill with a faded photograph, a warm cup, and a blank page.

Three times of a life as three objects. Not a knotted string timeline, not tickets on a line, not two copies of chapter one.

## Part 11 — Radical Curiosity and the Surprising Stuff

Entries 081–088. Theme: an unlabeled drawer and one honest surprise.

Accent: copper pull, teal shell.

**Art brief:** Draw a narrow library cabinet of unlabeled drawers, one drawer open on a single seashell and nothing else, in ink line with a copper drawer-pull and a teal shell.

Alt: Part opener for Radical Curiosity and the Surprising Stuff: a cabinet of unlabeled drawers, one open to reveal a single seashell.

Curiosity, not a hunt. Not a question box, not three doors under the word if, not a staircase of question marks.

## Part 12 — Building a Closer Future Together

Entries 089–096. Theme: a direction two people can see, still in pencil.

Accent: gold horizon, copper pencil.

**Art brief:** Draw a wide dawn window with two chairs facing it and a pencil path on the glass from the sill toward a low sun, in ink line with a gold horizon and a copper pencil resting on the sill.

Alt: Part opener for Building a Closer Future Together: two chairs at a dawn window and a pencil path drawn on the glass toward the sun.

A horizon, not a packed suitcase, not a blank constitution, not a kite sky, not a next-chapter page.

## Part 13 — The Inventor’s Workshop: Original Proposals

Entries 097–108. Theme: original experiences made by hand, on a bench, tonight.

Accent: copper type, midnight shadow under the bench.

**Art brief:** Draw a night workbench with a small screw press, a tray of blank cards, a stick of copper type, and one finished card standing up to dry, in ink line with a midnight shadow under the bench.

Alt: Part opener for The Inventor’s Workshop: a workbench with a screw press, blank cards, copper type, and one card drying.

A press and cards. Not an escape-room corridor, not an archaeologist’s brush, not a repair bench with thread, not a pile of building parts.
