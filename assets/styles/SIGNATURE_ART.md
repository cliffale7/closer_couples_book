## S01 — The Long Table

An escalating evening of questions. The table is long enough that the far end is deeper than the near end, and either person can stop.

Accent: copper lamps, dimmer as they recede. Midnight room, parchment cards.

**Art brief:** Draw a long table receding into a midnight room, place cards growing more personal with distance, lamps lower toward the far end, and two chairs at the near end turned slightly toward each other, with one card at the far place still face down.

Alt: Hero for The Long Table: a long table receding into a dark room, lowering lamps, deepening place cards, and one card left face down.

Not the Part 01 library table. That opener is two lamps and a run of blank pages. This is one set table and an evening that can go further or not. No banquet crowd, no heart confetti.

## S02 — The Night With the Lights Low

A sensory, romantic night for adults. Heat lives in the room after the lights change, and the door can still close.

Accent: burgundy throw. One lamp only. Gold is a thin rim on a glass, not a sparkle field.

**Art brief:** Draw a sitting room after the lights are down to one lamp, a record waiting on the player, a burgundy throw over a chair, two glasses on a low table, and a black window, with no people and no bed.

Alt: Hero for The Night With the Lights Low: a dim sitting room, one lamp, a waiting record, a burgundy throw, and a dark window.

Not the Part 05 hallway. That opener stops at the threshold. This hero is the room itself, still unoccupied. Mature, not explicit, not a clinch, not the chapter still life of citrus, cloth, and a shopping list of senses.

## S03 — The Case of the Missing Tuesday

A cooperative parlor mystery. A day is missing from the week, and the two people solve it by talking. It is unrelated to any scavenger hunt.

Accent: teal evening in the window, copper on the teapot. The blank Tuesday is parchment inside a charcoal calendar.

**Art brief:** Draw a parlor at evening with two armchairs, a teapot, and a wall calendar whose Tuesday square is blank, a coat over one chair and a single receipt on the table, arranged as a conversation rather than a trail.

Alt: Hero for The Case of the Missing Tuesday: a parlor, two armchairs, a teapot, and a calendar with Tuesday left blank.

Domestic objects in one room. No magnifying-glass path, no X on a map, no hidden prize, no stamps to collect, no outdoor clue route.

## S04 — The Branching Evening

A choose-your-own evening. Three rooms are visible from a hall, and the hall is a place to turn around.

Accent: three different lights — copper in the kitchen, teal in the walk, gold on the quiet chair. Midnight hall.

**Art brief:** Draw a house hall at night opening onto three rooms at once, a lit kitchen, a dark walk with a coat, and a quiet chair under a small lamp, the hall itself empty so two people can choose or turn back.

Alt: Hero for The Branching Evening: a night hall opening onto a kitchen, a dark walk, and a quiet chair, with room to turn around.

Not a forked path in a field, not three labeled game doors, not a dungeon. The rooms are ordinary and the choice is the picture.

## S05 — The Capsule We Open Later

A relationship time capsule made at the table and opened on a later day. The key stays with the couple. Nothing is buried and nothing is hunted.

Accent: copper clasp, a thin gold date line. Parchment papers, midnight only in the window.

**Art brief:** Draw a small sealed capsule on a library table, a short date scratched on the lid as line marks, with a photograph, a folded letter, and a key laid beside it for a day that is not tonight.

Alt: Hero for The Capsule We Open Later: a sealed capsule on a table, dated, with a photograph, a letter, and a key beside it.

The date is drawn as a mark, not as live text that must be read to use the session; the chapter prints the real date line. Not a treasure chest in the ground, not a map, not the Part 13 screw press.

## S06 — The Long Toast

A long celebration of years already lived and a year that has not arrived. Glasses stand in for time. It is not a wedding.

Accent: gold rims used sparingly, burgundy in one glass only, midnight window.

**Art brief:** Draw a sideboard of unmatched glasses in a long row like years, one glass raised, a night window behind them, and a blank card at the far end for the year that has not come.

Alt: Hero for The Long Toast: a row of unmatched glasses like years, one glass raised, a night window, and a blank card at the far end.

Unmatched glassware, not a matched champagne tower, not a cake, not place cards (those belong to S01). The blank card is the future, and it is visibly blank.
